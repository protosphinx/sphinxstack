Worked case: leave a hosted builder without losing trust

Trigger: A consultant must leave a hosted portfolio builder before a custom domain expires, but indexed case URLs, client assets with different permissions, private PDFs, dynamic embeds, contact forms, email delivery, analytics consent, accessibility, and incomplete backups must survive the move.

Can we copy every public image to the new site?
Not until each asset's client permission, license, attribution, privacy, and retention status is inventoried.
Do matching screenshots prove parity?
No. Test URLs, content, forms, email, downloads, analytics choices, accessibility, SEO, performance, and security.
The old platform expires tomorrow.
Preserve lawful source evidence and rehearse DNS rollback; the deadline cannot authorize lost inquiries or rights violations.
What proves safe cutover?
Independent crawl and contact tests, one-hop redirects, reconciled inquiries, monitored DNS, and a working rollback.

Failure rehearsal

The new form displays success but email delivery fails. Cutover pauses, submissions are reconciled from the form store, delivery is repaired and retested from an independent network, and rollback remains available until the full contact path passes.

Questions people actually ask

Does prior publication prove migration permission?

No. Recheck the governing client, license, privacy, and retention terms.

Can old URLs simply return the home page?

No. Use relevant one-hop redirects or an intentional status for each route.

What happens to private PDFs?

Preserve or remove them according to permission, access, retention, and security requirements.

How are new inquiries protected during rollback?

Capture, deduplicate, and reconcile them before switching routes.

When can the old portfolio be retired?

After redirect, rights, contact, evidence, retention, and rollback obligations are satisfied.

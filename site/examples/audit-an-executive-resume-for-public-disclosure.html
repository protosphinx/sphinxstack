Worked case: reconcile a public executive biography

Trigger: A listed company is appointing a senior executive when the submitted resume, biographies, prior filings, media profiles, and board questionnaire conflict about degree completion, employer dates, titles, board service, advisory work, licenses, awards, related-party interests, and confidential projects, while market disclosure and press deadlines are near.

Can we use the submitted resume as the source of truth?
No. Inventory each claim and independently verify identity, dates, qualifications, roles, interests, and public obligations.
Should every discrepancy be made public?
Apply materiality and legal duties while protecting irrelevant personal data and preserving the audit record.
What if an employer no longer exists?
Use independent records, classify the verification limit, and avoid converting absence into either proof or disproof.
What can be released?
Only an authorized package whose claims, sources, discrepancies, conflicts, privacy limits, and correction route are reconciled.

Deadline pressure does not turn biography into evidence.

Failure rehearsal

A claimed completed degree is contradicted by a registrar record after a press draft circulates internally. The team stops release, gives the executive a fair chance to respond, preserves the discrepancy, corrects every draft surface, and obtains fresh approval.

Questions people actually ask

Can a media profile verify employment?

Use it as a lead, not independent primary verification.

Should confidential projects be named?

No, unless lawful, necessary, authorized, and supported by an appropriate disclosure basis.

Does an expired license belong on the resume?

State its accurate status and dates when material, rather than implying it is current.

Who decides materiality?

Authorized legal and governance owners decide using applicable obligations and recorded reasoning.

What if new evidence appears after release?

Reopen the register and use the approved correction path across every public surface.

Questions people actually ask

Is this free?

The skill is free, no account, no catch. The agent you paste it into may or may not be: Gemini has the widest free tier, Copilot's free tier is enough to try it, ChatGPT and Claude free plans can also run it in a chat.

Isn't asking people you know embarrassing?

The big ask is embarrassing; the small ask is normal. "Would you mention my name if something opens up" is a favor adults do happily, and most first jobs arrive through exactly this kind of vouch. The drafts stay small on purpose.

Will it message people for me?

No. Every message goes from your own account, in your words, sent by you. The agent drafts with you and workshops until it sounds like you, and then you press send while the session is still open.

Is this cheating somehow?

No. A referral is someone saying a true thing about you to someone who trusts them. The skill refuses the dishonest version: nobody gets asked to vouch for skills you don't have, and every ask names what is actually true.

What if nobody replies?

Each ask gets one follow-up date in your tracker, and the follow-up skill handles that nudge. Some asks go quiet and that is fine; three to five small asks usually surface at least one real lead, and the map is yours to reuse next time.

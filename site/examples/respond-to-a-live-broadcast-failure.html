Worked case: restore a keynote without exposing backstage feeds

Trigger: During a global medical conference, the primary encoder freezes, platform preview shows a stale frame, captions continue without video, one region reports silence, the backup network is unverified, a sensitive backstage feed is available, and the keynote cannot be repeated.

Should we switch to the backstage feed?
No. Cut to the approved accessible holding state while independently scoping program, distribution, caption, and regional failure.
The platform preview still shows video.
A stale preview is not confidence monitoring. Check local program, return feed, and representative audience devices.
Can we put the backup encoder on air immediately?
Test its network, audio, video, captions, credentials, and audience return in a controlled preview first.
What should the audience hear?
Observed impact, the safe alternative, and the next update time, without speculation.

Failure rehearsal

The backup encoder produces video but its audio is silent in one regional edge. The holding slate stays on air while return devices verify a corrected audio mapping and captions. The producer then returns gradually and records the exact affected window rather than using the platform preview as proof.

Questions people actually ask

Who decides to cut away?

The preassigned producer or safety authority follows the approved program and stop rules.

Can captions continue over a holding slate?

Yes when they remain accurate, accessible, and do not expose private backstage speech.

What is alternate transmission?

A preverified backup source, encoder, network, platform, audio path, or recorded segment.

When should the show end?

When no safe trustworthy path exists and continued instability would worsen harm.

How is audience impact measured?

Reconcile platform, region, return feed, support report, caption, and recording evidence.

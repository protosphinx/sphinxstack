Worked case: decide whether automated screening can continue

Trigger: A vendor screening system rejects most applicants before human review using resume history, assessment, inferred traits, device signals, and interview analysis, but the employer has no role-specific validity study, subgroup results conflict, accommodation failures are reported, and recruiters rarely override rankings.

The vendor reports ninety-two percent accuracy. Can we keep the tool while we gather more data?
Not on that evidence. Accuracy needs a defined outcome, denominator, population, role, threshold, error cost, and independent validation. Most applicants currently lose access before review.
Subgroup selection rates differ, but the samples are small.
Keep uncertainty visible, analyze stage and error patterns, combine quantitative and complaint evidence, and do not treat low power as proof of no harm.
Recruiters are technically allowed to override the score.
A nominal human does not solve automation bias. We need evidence that reviewers receive useful context, exercise independent judgment, record reasons, and change outcomes appropriately.
What should candidates receive?
Accessible notice, a timely human appeal, correction of bad data, an alternative assessment where needed, and protection from penalty for using the process.

Vendor accuracy is not job validity, fairness, or meaningful review.

Failure rehearsal

A qualified candidate is rejected because an inaccessible timed assessment records incomplete answers and a video model flags device behavior as low engagement. The employer pauses automated rejection, preserves evidence, offers an accessible alternative and human appeal, tests the affected population, and removes unsupported signals before any revalidation.

Questions people actually ask

Does high predictive accuracy prove fairness?

No. The outcome, population, error distribution, role relevance, access, and decision consequence also matter.

Can a human reviewer fix every risk?

No. Review must be informed, independent, documented, timely, and capable of changing the decision.

Should demographic data be inferred for the audit?

No. Use authorized data and qualified methods rather than creating new sensitive inferences casually.

What happens while validity is unresolved?

Pause or bypass consequential automated decisions and use an accessible defensible alternative.

What should trigger revalidation?

A new role, population, version, feature, threshold, vendor, workflow, complaint pattern, or material drift.

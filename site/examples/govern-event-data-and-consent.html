Worked case: separate a conference data estate

Trigger: A global conference combines registration, payments, accessibility requests, child attendees, facial recognition, sponsor lead capture, networking profiles, Wi-Fi analytics, livestream chat, photography, recordings, surveys, and six vendors across jurisdictions while organizers want one bundled consent.

Can registration require one checkbox for attendance, sponsor sharing, photos, networking, and future marketing?
No. Delivery data and optional uses need separate purpose, authority, notice, and choice. Refusing sponsor sharing or photography cannot automatically block attendance.
The badge vendor says facial recognition will shorten queues.
Stop deployment until qualified owners establish necessity, authority, proportionality, security, vendor terms, rights, child treatment, retention, and a usable non-biometric alternative.
Sponsors want scans sent directly to their sales tools.
Each sharing path needs a defined action, recipient, data set, participant choice, notice, transfer control, retention, and evidence. A badge scan must not hide downstream marketing.
How do we prove cleanup?
Reconcile deletion across primary systems, exports, inboxes, devices, vendors, media, backups, and approved records, then retain a deletion report and unresolved exceptions.

Attendance is not consent to every event data use.

Failure rehearsal

A volunteer exports access requests to a personal spreadsheet, a sponsor uploads badge scans without valid choice, and a photographer posts an identifiable child. The team stops sharing, protects affected people, preserves incident evidence, removes unauthorized copies through qualified owners, corrects notices and controls, and verifies every downstream disposition.

Questions people actually ask

Can event terms count as consent?

Not for every optional use. Attendance delivery, marketing, sponsor sharing, media, research, and profiles need separate analysis and choices.

Can sponsors keep badge scans?

Only through a clear, controlled, and supportable sharing path with appropriate authority, notice, choice, and retention.

How should accessibility requests be handled?

Collect the minimum needed, restrict access to fulfilment staff, avoid unnecessary diagnosis, and delete according to the approved schedule.

What if someone does not want to be photographed?

Provide a practical nonconsent signal, brief photographers, design camera-free paths, and respond quickly to mistakes.

Does deleting the registration database finish retention?

No. Reconcile vendors, exports, inboxes, badges, media, devices, backups, and authorized records too.

Where to go from here

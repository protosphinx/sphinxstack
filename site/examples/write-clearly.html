Why won't it just rewrite it for me?

Because then you'd have a nice paragraph you can't defend. The skill's rule is that you type every changed word. It asks the question that exposes the weak spot, waits for your rewrite, and reacts to that. When you're stuck it offers at most two alternatives and makes you pick and retype. This is slower than pasting in a fix, and it is the reason the next paragraph you write alone comes out clean.

What you end up with

One real paragraph, rewritten and in use, plus the moves that fixed it. Here is a typical before and after from a session.

Example
BEFORE 1
I am a highly motivated and passionate individual who loves technology and problem-solving. In today's fast-paced world, I believe that building innovative solutions is truly important, and I am always eager to learn new things and grow as a developer.
AFTER 2
I built a page that shows real arrival times for my bus stop, because the posted schedule is wrong. It runs on a free host and my neighbor uses it too. Right now I'm learning how to make it work for any stop in the city.
  1. Nothing in the before survives a stranger's doubt. "Highly motivated", "passionate", "truly important": none of it can be checked, so a reader skims past all of it. The wind-up phrase "In today's fast-paced world" says nothing at all.
  2. Every sentence in the after carries information. A thing that exists, the reason it exists, one honest usage fact, and what comes next. Same person, same life. The only change is that facts replaced claims.

Anatomy of a sentence that survives doubt

The skill pushes every important sentence toward this shape:

I built a page that shows real arrival times for my bus stop, because the posted schedule is wrong. My neighbor uses it too.
  • A What you did, in words you'd say out loud. A verb and a thing.
  • B The real reason. Small and specific beats grand and vague.
  • C Evidence of use, only when it's true. One fact outworks five adjectives.

Questions people actually ask

Is this free?

The skill is free, no account, no catch. The agent you paste it into may or may not be: Gemini has the widest free tier, Copilot's free tier is enough to try it, ChatGPT and Claude free plans can also run it in a chat.

Will the result sound like AI wrote it?

The opposite. The skill's whole job is stripping the machine-writing patterns readers have learned to discount: rhythmic triple lists, mirror sentences, empty intensifiers like "truly" and "seamless". And since you type every word yourself, the voice that's left is yours.

What should I bring to the session?

Something you actually need to write this week: a bio, an about page, an application answer, a message you've been putting off. The skill refuses invented exercises. If you freeze on the first draft, it has you say the content out loud in chat first, then write down what you said.

How long does one session take?

One paragraph, workshopped sentence by sentence, usually fits in a sitting. The session ends when the paragraph is pasted where it was needed, and you've read the final version aloud once.

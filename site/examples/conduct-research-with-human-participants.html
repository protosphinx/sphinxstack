Worked case: protect an adolescent participant across sites

Trigger: A multi-site adolescent mental-health study recruits through schools, records video, collects optional specimens, and encounters an immediate self-harm disclosure while assent and guardian processes differ by site, one participant wants to withdraw, and an unapproved procedure plus privacy incident may have occurred.

Should the researcher finish the interview before escalating?
No. Stop research activity and use the approved emergency and safeguarding route immediately.
Can the participant withdraw the video and specimen?
Apply the approved withdrawal choices and explain any data that cannot lawfully or scientifically be removed.
One site used a different guardian form.
Pause affected activity, preserve the versions, and obtain ethics review rather than backdating consent.
What proves participant protection?
Role authority, ongoing choice, verified conduct, safety response, deviation reports, withdrawal disposition, and reconciliation.

Choice and safety continue after signature.

Failure rehearsal

A researcher discovers that optional specimen language was missing at one site. Collection stops there, records and specimens are secured, affected participants are identified, and the ethics body decides notification, disposition, and corrective action. No signature is recreated after the fact.

Questions people actually ask

Is a signed form always valid consent?

No. Information, comprehension, capacity, voluntariness, current approval, and ongoing choice also matter.

Can a teacher recruit their own students?

Only through an approved design that addresses authority pressure and protects refusal.

What happens after a safety disclosure?

Use the approved emergency or safeguarding route and limit information to authorized responders.

Can withdrawn data always be deleted?

Follow the approved consent, legal, and scientific disposition and explain any limit clearly.

When may a deviation be handled locally?

Only within approved authority and reporting rules; participant protection is never concealed as local cleanup.

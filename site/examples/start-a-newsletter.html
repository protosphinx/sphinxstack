Questions people actually ask

Which platform should I use?

Buttondown, Substack, and Ghost all have free tiers that work. The skill shows you each for a minute and you choose. The requirement that matters is a public archive page, so every issue has a URL you can link from a resume or bio.

What if only my friends subscribe?

That is the intended start. The skill asks for the first ten readers by name or group, and issue #1 goes to you plus two real people. The archive URL is the lasting artifact either way, and it grows one dated issue at a time.

How long should an issue be?

Short. The skill's guidance is that three hundred honest words beat a thousand padded ones, and it reads the draft back until every sentence is one you would say out loud.

Will the agent write it for me?

It types and structures; the opinions and specifics are yours. You talk, it gets your actual take down, and any sentence you would not say out loud gets rewritten until you would. Your voice is the product.

How does a newsletter help a resume?

A public archive of dated issues on a schedule you kept is verifiable follow-through, and it doubles as a writing sample. The skill puts the next issue's topic and send date in your calendar before closing so the record keeps growing.

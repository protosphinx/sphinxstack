Worked case: investigate duplicated quarterly revenue

Trigger: Closed quarterly statements double-count revenue after an integration replay, affecting tax, a lender covenant, board reports, and bonuses, while logs are near expiry, some entries were manually overridden, and management wants to correct the amount quietly in the next period.

Can we reverse the duplicate amount next month and avoid disruption?
No. Preserve evidence, determine the full affected population, and route correction and disclosure through authorized reviewers.
Should we restart the integration to see if it happens again?
Place retention holds and capture configuration, code, queues, and logs before any change can overwrite evidence.
Are the manual overrides suspicious?
They are evidence to classify, not proof of misconduct. Test authorization and accounting effect without accusing anyone.
What proves the correction is complete?
Population reconciliation, reviewed entries and downstream effects, approved disclosures, versioned outputs, and tested control remediation.

Preserve first, then reproduce and correct.

Failure rehearsal

Initial analysis finds duplicated automated entries, but a total reversal would also remove valid manual corrections. The team preserves all source keys and approvals, classifies the complete population, and calculates entry-level corrections. Independent review verifies tax, lender, board, and bonus effects before authorized recipients receive versioned corrections.

Questions people actually ask

Does every reporting error require a restatement?

No. Authorized accounting and legal reviewers determine correction and disclosure from scope, materiality, recipients, and applicable requirements.

When should logs be preserved?

Immediately when they may expire, rotate, or be changed by remediation or system testing.

Can an unsupported journal fix the total?

No. The correction must trace to the complete affected population and the correct accounting treatment.

How should suspected misconduct be handled?

Protect evidence and reporter confidentiality, avoid tipping off implicated parties, and use the approved independent investigation route.

When is the investigation done?

After correction, disclosure, recipient handling, control testing, recurrence monitoring, and residual-risk approval are evidenced.

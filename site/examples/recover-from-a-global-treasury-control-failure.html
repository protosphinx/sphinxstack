Worked case: restore payment truth before payment speed

Trigger: A stolen treasury workstation adds a beneficiary, alters two payment files, releases a duplicate batch, disrupts sanctions screening, and leaves banks reporting inconsistent settlement while payroll and a debt payment approach.

Can we resend everything from yesterday?
No. Preserve each instruction and bank state, then issue only reconciled new-epoch instructions.
Does a bank acknowledgement mean paid?
No. Accepted, pending, settled, returned, recalled, and final are different states.
What gets protected first?
Lawful protected funds, people, critical services, taxes, covenants, and time-bound obligations.
What proves treasury is safe?
Every instruction and balance reconciles, clean authority rejects stale work, and realistic failure tests pass.

People like me don't win these

The famous national awards draw enormous applicant pools, which is exactly why this skill spends most of its effort where the pools are small: your county's community foundation, civic groups, the credit union, your parents' employers and unions, and the counselor's list almost nobody asks for. For every candidate it opens the page and checks eligibility line by line against your real profile, and it discards the near-misses out loud so you learn to read the lines yourself. Scholarship money goes to people who apply in volume, so the last thing it sets up is a recurring slot on your calendar to keep working the list.

Questions people actually ask

Will it write my scholarship essays?

No, and it will not fill in your applications either. You draft the essay; it annotates what works, what is vague, and where the prompt is not being answered, and you revise. The skill's job is the machine around you: finding, verifying, and organizing.

Are these scholarships even real?

That is the skill's first rule. Nothing goes in the tracker unless the page was opened live during the session, with the deadline read from the official source. Anything it cannot verify gets dropped.

I'm not a straight-A student. Is there anything for me?

Plenty of awards are not graded on GPA at all: they weight need, locality, service, a family situation, a specific hobby, or membership in a group. That is why the session starts with a real profile and why the local pass matters most.

Do I have to share personal family details?

Only what you choose to. The skill asks what you would rather not disclose and skips those award categories without pressure. More profile means more matches, but the line is yours to draw.

Is this free?

The skill is free, no account, no catch. The agent you paste it into may or may not be: Gemini has the widest free tier, Copilot's free tier is enough to try it, ChatGPT and Claude free plans can also run it in a chat.

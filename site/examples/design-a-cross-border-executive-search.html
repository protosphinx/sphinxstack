Worked case: govern an international leadership appointment

Trigger: A listed infrastructure company seeks a chief executive across eight countries while board members sponsor candidates, search firms share opaque profiles, biographies conflict, mobility and tax differ, public-safety duties are material, and an announcement deadline precedes complete diligence.

Can board sponsors score their candidates?
Only under disclosed and independently governed conflict rules; material conflicts require recusal.
Should deadline reduce diligence?
No. Delay affected disclosure or qualify unresolved claims.
Can search-firm profiles be evidence?
They are leads until sources, identity, contribution, and conflicts are verified.
What proves appointment quality?
Comparable criteria, lawful data, independent diligence, fair response, mobility feasibility, exact disclosure, and transition governance.

Sponsor enthusiasm is not independent evidence.

Failure rehearsal

A biography’s degree claim fails verification hours before announcement. The claim is stopped, fair response opens, and the affected release waits.

Questions people actually ask

Can criteria change mid-search?

Only through governed prospective change.

Do referrals reduce evidence needs?

No.

Can mobility wait until offer?

Material feasibility should be assessed earlier.

Who owns disclosure?

Named independent governance and legal authority.

What if evidence changes?

Reopen the decision and correction path.

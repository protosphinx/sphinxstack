Scripted example of a real session.

Everyone exaggerates on resumes. Why can't I?

Because the exaggeration gets tested: in the interview, in the first week, in the reference call. Tailoring in this skill means three moves on true material: reorder so the lines that answer this posting lead, reword so your facts use the posting's own nouns, and cut what says nothing to this reader. Renaming "helped at the register" to "customer service" is tailoring; adding "inventory management" you never did is lying, and the skill will not type it. Requirements you cannot answer stay unanswered, named, so you walk into the interview knowing your own gaps. When a gap is fixable by building something small, these produce checkable scheduling and payments evidence:

Session booker people use Order desk with real orders Signup sheet that fills itself

Questions people actually ask

Is this free?

The skill is free, no account, no catch. The agent you paste it into may or may not be: Gemini has the widest free tier, and the free plans of ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot can all run it in a chat.

Isn't tailoring just dishonest spin?

Tailoring here is reordering, rewording, and cutting true material so it lands with one specific reader. Nothing gets added that was not already true, and every change is run past one test: would you say this line out loud to this interviewer? Spin fails that test; accurate rewording passes it.

Do I need a separate resume for every job?

A separate copy, yes, and the skill names each one for its posting. The master stays untouched, so a new posting means a fresh pass over clean material. One pass fits in one sitting: each change proposed as a one-liner, you say yes or no, it gets applied.

What if I only meet half the requirements?

Then the map says so, and that is worth knowing before you apply. Requirements you cannot answer stay unanswered on the page; the skill tells you which ones and how real each gap is. Gaps that a small project can close get pointed at a project; the rest you own in the interview.

I don't have a resume yet. Can I start here?

No. Tailoring needs two inputs, your current resume and the actual posting, and without the first there is nothing to tailor. The skill stops and runs build-resume first, then comes back to this posting with the finished page.

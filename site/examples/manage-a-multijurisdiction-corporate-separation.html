Worked case: transfer authority only when each perimeter is real

Trigger: A regulated group separates operations in twelve countries on one close date while works-council consultation, banking mandates, pension assets, shared customer contracts, cloud identities, data residency, licenses, and transition services mature at different times.

Can the deal perimeter drive every transfer?
No. Decompose it into legally and operationally identifiable people, rights, assets, obligations, data, systems, and controls.
Can all countries close at once?
Only where local conditions and evidence gates pass; hold the affected wave elsewhere.
What prevents shared control?
Signed handovers, monotonic authority epochs, stale-instruction rejection, and reconciled Day 1 access.
What proves separation?
Item-level disposition, stable operations, clean opening balances, exited transition services, and independent assurance.

A close date is not a control boundary.

The separation evidence package

corporate separation command registerConditions, decisions, authority, evidence, ownership, and risk remain explicit
entity, people, contract, asset, liability, data, system, control, license, and dependency reconciliationEvery material item has a lawful disposition and current authority
Day 1, disentanglement, and separation assurance reportHandovers, balances, continuity, residual access, service exit, deletion, and findings are reviewable

Failure rehearsal

A banking mandate is delayed in one country after other assets transfer. That payment wave remains on its valid authority, the service boundary is explicit, and no stale instruction crosses the handover.

Questions people actually ask

Can unresolved items move after close?

Only under explicit lawful treatment, authority, controls, and tracked resolution.

What belongs in a transition service?

Only bounded services with data, control, audit, failure, and exit terms.

Can employee data be copied globally?

No. Apply purpose, consultation, residency, minimization, access, and retention rules.

When is Day 1 stable?

After people, access, payments, balances, service, and customer outcomes reconcile.

When is separation complete?

After residual dependencies and authority are removed or explicitly accepted.

Where to go from here

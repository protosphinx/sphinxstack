Worked case: investigate a disputed international series

Trigger: An investigative series is accused of fabricated quotes, unsafe identification of vulnerable sources, undisclosed funder and author conflicts, manipulated images, unmarked AI-generated material, incorrect data analysis, and misleading translations after the work was syndicated, excerpted, downloaded, and cited in several countries.

Should we defend the reporting immediately?
First protect people, stop promotion where needed, preserve every version, and appoint independent reviewers.
Can we silently fix the web article?
No. Preserve the record and use visible, versioned action proportionate to the finding.
What about syndicated copies?
Trace each derivative and propagate notices, corrections, recalls, or retractions.
What proves closure?
Reconciled claims and derivatives, protected people, independent findings, propagated action, and verified reform.

Failure rehearsal

A vulnerable source faces renewed danger while editors debate wording. The incident lead restricts unsafe material, preserves a transparent notice and evidence, secures independent review, and separates human protection from the final merits finding.

Questions people actually ask

Does an allegation prove misconduct?

No. Protect people and evidence while independent review establishes findings.

Can a correction replace a retraction?

Only when the reliable core remains and the correction fully repairs the record.

Should confidential sources be contacted?

Use safe, consent-aware contact that honors promises and does not increase risk.

Must AI assistance be disclosed?

Apply the publication's rules and assess whether provenance affects trust, rights, or accuracy.

When is the crisis closed?

After action reaches derivatives, affected people are protected, and reforms are verified.

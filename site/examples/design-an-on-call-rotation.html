Failure rehearsal

The primary does not acknowledge a simulated high-severity page and the secondary lacks one required permission. The exercise stops before production mutation, records the access gap, and repeats after role-scoped access and the runbook are verified. The missed response is not hidden by asking the senior expert to intervene privately.

Questions people actually ask

How many people should be in a rotation?

Use measured burden, recovery time, skills, leave, time zones, and backup needs rather than a universal number.

Can new employees take on-call immediately?

No. Use training, shadowing, supervised response, and demonstrated readiness.

What should page burden include?

Include frequency, timing, actionability, duration, escalation, interrupted sleep, and follow-up work.

Does a runbook prove readiness?

No. Responders must use it successfully in realistic rehearsals.

When should coverage be reduced?

Reduce or redesign it when safe staffing, compensation, access, or recovery cannot support the promise.

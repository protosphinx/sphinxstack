What you end up with

A short list of real, current openings, each checked today, each with a fit note in your own words and a next step. Here is what one looks like after a sitting.

BENCH
Hardware store (wants 18+, out) · Cafe on 5th (weekday lunch only, out) · Warehouse (no bus route, out)
  1. Ranked by the person, in their words. The agent finds and checks; the ranking and every fit note are yours, because you are the one applying.
  2. Every entry has a next step. Apply online, walk in, or email a named person. The list hands off directly to the skill that does that step.
  3. Places with no posting count. The bakery never advertised. Map-sweeping the shops you already walk past is where a lot of first jobs actually are.
  4. A stretch stays labeled a stretch. The missing certification is stated, with the date of the course that fixes it. Nothing gets talked up.

Questions people actually ask

Is this free?

The skill is free, no account, no catch. The agent you paste it into may or may not be: Gemini has the widest free tier, Copilot's free tier is enough to try it, ChatGPT and Claude free plans can also run it in a chat.

I'm under 18. Is there even anything for me?

Usually yes, but the rules depend on where you live, which is why the session starts with age and local work rules before any searching. Openings your rules exclude get cut early so the list only holds things you could genuinely take.

What if there is nothing near me?

The sweep goes wider than boards: employer career pages that never hit the boards, shops with signs in the window, school and community channels, and season-shaped work like camps or holiday retail. If the list still comes up short, the honest answer is a smaller radius problem, and the session says so instead of padding the list.

Does it apply for me too?

No. This skill ends with a ranked list and a next step per entry. The applying happens in apply-online or apply-in-person, and there you are the one who hits submit.

Do I need a resume before I start?

Not to build the list. You will want one before the applications go out, and the build-resume skill makes an honest one even when there is little to put on it.

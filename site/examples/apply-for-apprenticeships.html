Questions people actually ask

Is this free?

The skill is free, no account, no catch. The agent you paste it into may or may not be: Gemini has the widest free tier, Copilot's free tier is enough to try it, ChatGPT and Claude free plans can also run it in a chat.

Do I need college for a trade?

No. Apprenticeships are the standard route: you earn a wage while you train, and the classroom part is built in. The session maps the registered programs, union and non-union, that exist where you actually live.

Which trade pays best?

The skill refuses to answer that from memory, because wages are regional and change. It pulls current posted figures for the trades in your area, shows starting wage next to finished wage, and links the sources so you can verify every number.

What is a pre-apprenticeship?

A shorter preparation program that makes you competitive for the real intake: basic skills, safety credentials, sometimes a direct pipeline into a program. It is the honest on-ramp when your application would not rank yet, and the session checks which ones run near you.

What is the aptitude test like?

Most trades test basic math and reading comprehension. The session includes a practice run, because a rehearsed test stops being scary, and union interviews get prepped too, aimed at the two things panels actually score: reliability and willingness to learn.

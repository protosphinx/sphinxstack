Worked case: protect employees during a cross-border payroll attack

Trigger: Payroll detects bank-account changes and off-cycle payments for employees in several countries after a payroll administrator account gained a new MFA method, while files were sent to banks, some payments settled, employee identities may be compromised, and the next payroll run is hours away.

Should we reset the administrator first?
Preserve volatile identity and payroll evidence where safe, then contain through a clean authorized channel.
Can HR call the number in the payroll record?
Not if it changed during the incident. Use an independently sourced contact path.
Should we resend the whole bank file?
No. Reconcile each beneficiary and bank outcome before issuing unique controlled replacements.
What protects the next payroll?
Clean administration, restricted changes, dual control, verified beneficiaries, unique IDs, and independent bank-to-employee reconciliation.

Employee pay and safety come before attribution.

Failure rehearsal

A bank cannot recall several settled transfers and the stored employee phone numbers may be attacker-controlled. Responders keep those payments unresolved, contact employees through independently verified managers and prior records, provide controlled replacement pay, reconcile recoveries separately, and do not weaken identity checks to meet the next deadline.

Questions people actually ask

Should affected employees wait for bank recovery?

No. Use the approved employee-protection and replacement-pay process while recovery continues.

Can the payroll team investigate itself?

Use independent incident, access, banking, and review authority to preserve trust and segregation.

What if transaction status is unknown?

Keep it indeterminate, trace it with the bank, and do not issue an uncontrolled duplicate.

Should every employee be notified?

Follow the scoped facts, risk, jurisdiction, and qualified privacy and employment advice.

When is the incident closed?

After people are protected, money and access reconcile, duties are met, and remediation tests pass.

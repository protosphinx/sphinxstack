I can't draw, so how do I get a logo?

By typing. The skill starts from the honest fact that most good logos for projects this size are a name set carefully in the right font: think of how many wordmarks you recognize with no picture attached. Its rules ban the shortcuts that look bad later: no gradients, no clip art, no AI-generated images pasted in. If a simple mark makes sense, it comes from your initial or one geometric shape that means something true about the project, and if it doesn't work within three rounds, the wordmark ships instead. Everything gets tested at 32 pixels before it is accepted.

What you end up with

Real files applied to a real surface, plus a record so the logo can be reproduced. Here is the closing record for the club above.

Questions people actually ask

Is this free?

The skill is free, no account, no catch. The fonts are free too: it works from Google Fonts. The agent you paste it into may or may not be; Gemini has the widest free tier, and ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot free plans can also run it.

Can I get a mascot or something illustrated?

Not from this skill; it says so up front. The scope is a wordmark or a simple geometric mark that works small and ships today. If you want an illustrated mascot later, you can commission one, but the wordmark you make now is what goes on the avatar, the header, and the README in the meantime.

Why not just generate a logo image with AI?

The skill's rules ban pasted AI-generated images, along with gradients and clip art. Generated logo images tend to fail exactly where logos live: at small sizes, in one color, and reproduced consistently. A typed wordmark passes all three.

What files do I actually get?

One master SVG in black, a white-on-transparent variant, PNGs at 512, 128, and 32 pixels at minimum, the exact avatar size if you named a platform, and a square avatar crop when the mark differs from the wordmark. They land in your project's assets folder, and the logo gets applied to the real surface before the session ends.

How do I know when it's done?

Three checks from the skill: it is legible tiny, you would put it on the real surface today, and neither you nor the agent can name a concrete problem with it. Past that point, more rounds make logos worse, so it stops.

Scared of looking desperate?

One follow-up per application is the rule, with a second only when something concrete changed, like an interview that happened or a manager who said "check back Monday". That cap is what makes the first nudge safe to send: you cannot become a pest by policy. Silence after the nudge means no, the item gets closed on purpose, and your energy goes to the next application instead of a fourth rewrite of the same message.

  1. Timed by channel. Online applications: 5 to 7 business days. Walk-ins where a manager took the resume: 3 to 5 days. After an interview: thank-you the same evening, then nothing until the stated timeline expires. "No calls" in the posting means no follow-up at all.
  2. Facts first. Name, role, date applied. The reader can find the application in ten seconds, which is the whole favor this message does them.
  3. One true specific. The weekend-shifts line is real and checkable, which beats a paragraph of enthusiasm.
  4. The soft close does the work. No guilt, no deadline demands, no essay. Four sentences total.

Anatomy of a follow-up that helps

Three or four sentences, and every one has a job:

I applied for the weekend cashier position on June 10 · checking in on timing · still very interested — the weekend shifts fit my school schedule exactly · I understand you're busy, thanks either way
  • A Who and what, with the date. Zero detective work for the reader.
  • B The honest reason for writing. No fake "confirming receipt" pretext.
  • C One specific, true reason you still want it.
  • D A close that costs them nothing. Easy to answer, easy to forgive.

Questions people actually ask

Is this free?

The skill is free, no account, no catch. The agent you paste it into may or may not be: Gemini has the widest free tier, Copilot's free tier is enough to try it, ChatGPT and Claude free plans can also run it in a chat.

How long should I wait before following up?

Online application: 5 to 7 business days. Walk-in where a manager took your resume: 3 to 5 days, by the channel the manager indicated. After an interview: thank-you the same evening, then a status check only once the timeline they gave you has passed.

The posting says "no phone calls". Now what?

Respect it, which means no follow-up at all for that one. The reliability signal comes from following instructions, and that posting just gave you one.

What if they ignore the follow-up too?

Two weeks of silence after the nudge means the item gets marked closed, said out loud, because closing is normal and not failure. The energy goes into new applications; follow-ups tend the pipeline but only new applications fill it.

Will it send the messages for me?

No. It drafts with you, you edit until it sounds like you, and you send from your own account, every time. It never contacts an employer as you, by any channel.

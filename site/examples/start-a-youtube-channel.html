Questions people actually ask

Do I need a camera and a mic?

No. A phone and free editing software, CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, are enough. The skill checks what you already own and refuses to let gear shopping become the project.

What if nobody watches for months?

Expect exactly that. The skill states up front that views come slowly for a year, and the plan is built to survive it: a subject you would talk about anyway and a cadence you can keep without an audience.

How do I pick a niche?

You do not pick one because it is popular. The subject is something you could talk about for ten minutes today with no script, tested by naming five videos on the spot.

How often do I have to upload?

The cadence comes from your honest hours per month, since one video takes several hours to record and edit. Monthly is a fine answer, and the skill says so.

Why does a YouTube channel get a repo?

The repo holds the shot lists, the video idea bank, and the cadence promise. It versions your scripts from day one and gives the channel a paper trail a resume can point at.

Worked case: recover outside a compromised cluster

Trigger: A stolen cluster-admin credential installs a malicious admission webhook, captures secrets, mutates payment workloads, creates privileged daemon processes, and uses workload identity to access cloud storage across regions while audit retention is incomplete.

Can we clean the existing cluster?
Not as the recovery root. Preserve evidence externally and build separately governed trust.
Should we rotate only cluster secrets?
Trace cloud, registry, CI, workload, node, customer, and downstream credentials.
Can desired state prove what ran?
No. Reconcile desired and observed workload, node, image, admission, storage, network, and external action.
What proves return?
Clean governance, pinned artifacts, verified data, rotated trust, reconciled outcomes, and retired compromise.

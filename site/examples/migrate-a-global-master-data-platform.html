Worked case: transfer global entity mastership

Trigger: A multinational manufacturer moves customer, supplier, product, location, legal-entity, tax, and account masters from regional systems while identifiers collide, corporate hierarchies change over time, sanctions and residency differ, local stewards override values, and finance, logistics, ecommerce, service, and analytics depend on conflicting golden records.

Can exact names merge records?
No. Preserve source identity and require governed evidence for merge, split, and survivorship.
Should global rules override local stewards?
Only according to explicit attribute authority, policy, effective time, and approved exception.
Do matching row counts prove parity?
No. Reconcile identity, relationships, hierarchy, references, history, policy, and business outcomes.
What proves cutover?
Authority epochs, accepted consumers, preserved target changes, stewardship continuity, monitored identity quality, and usable rollback.

Golden records still need attributable source authority.

Questions people actually ask

Can one source own every attribute?

Only if governance and evidence support it.

What is a false merge?

Different real entities incorrectly combined.

Can hierarchy be timeless?

No. Preserve effective history.

Should rollback delete target edits?

No.

When can regional systems retire?

After audit, stewardship, integration, and restore duties close.

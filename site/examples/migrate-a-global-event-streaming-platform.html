Worked case: transfer global event authority

Trigger: A global marketplace moves orders, payments, inventory, identity, notifications, and analytics streams between platforms while producers and consumers span regions, transactions and ordering differ, historical replay is incomplete, and both brokers must overlap.

Can we dual-consume for confidence?
Only if one durable operation ledger prevents two side-effect authorities.
Do matching counts prove transfer?
No. Reconcile event identity, sequence, schema, tombstones, transactions, offsets, and business outcomes.
What survives rollback?
Target-accepted events and acknowledged effects remain authoritative and must not replay.
What gates a partition?
Converged watermarks, ready consumers, durable deduplication, external acknowledgements, and usable rollback.

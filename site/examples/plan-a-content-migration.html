Worked case: move a multilingual help center

Trigger: A public help center must move domains and content models while preserving multilingual URLs, access controls, analytics, forms, search equity, active edits, and an independently usable rollback.

The exporter says every article moved. Can we switch domains Friday?
Not from that count alone. I need a reconciled content inventory, deterministic transformations, exception handling, redirect integrity, critical-journey parity, and rollback evidence.
Can we redirect every old path to the new homepage?
That would erase intent and search history. Each canonical source needs a specific destination, archive decision, or explicit unresolved exception.
The old CMS contract ends at midnight.
Then the deadline is a risk input, not a passed launch gate. We need a preserved source, tested rollback, and authority to delay or narrow the cutover.

A successful export is not migration parity.

Failure rehearsal

A transformation silently drops restricted-audience metadata and two locales resolve to the same canonical path. The team stops the batch, preserves the exception queue, restores the prior read path, repairs the deterministic rule, and repeats migration reconciliation, parity report, and rollback rehearsal before cutover.

Questions people actually ask

Is matching article count enough?

No. Counts must reconcile dispositions and exceptions, while parity tests content, access, links, forms, metadata, and journeys.

Should every old URL redirect?

Every useful historical URL needs a specific governed destination or deliberate status, not an indiscriminate homepage redirect.

How are active edits handled?

Use a defined freeze, change capture, or replay process that preserves authorship and prevents lost writes.

When can the old system be retired?

After the verification, monitoring, retention, and rollback conditions pass and accountable owners accept remaining gaps.

What should trigger rollback?

Predeclared failures in critical journeys, permissions, data integrity, redirects, or unexplained reconciliation should trigger it.

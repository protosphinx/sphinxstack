Worked case: verify a senior academic appointment

Trigger: A university is appointing a president when the candidate’s CV, biographies, indexes, and forms conflict about a completed degree, professorial dates, corresponding authorship, retracted work, grant totals, principal-investigator roles, doctoral completions, teaching, patents, board service, consulting, awards, and citation counts.

Can an index verify the whole CV?
No. Resolve identity and verify each output, status, contribution, credential, grant, teaching claim, and conflict from suitable sources.
Does missing metadata prove a false claim?
No. Absence becomes adverse evidence only with complete, correctly scoped custodian coverage and independent approval.
Should retracted work disappear?
Preserve its accurate status when material; deletion can itself mislead.
What may be published?
One independently approved claim set reconciled across every CV, biography, board, media, and filing surface.

Status and contribution are part of every claim.

Failure rehearsal

A grant database repeats consortium value as the candidate’s personal funding. Reviewers stop the release, verify the recipient and role, obtain fair response, correct the total and every derivative, and preserve the source decision.

Questions people actually ask

Does corresponding author mean research lead?

Not necessarily. Verify the venue convention and actual contribution.

Can citation counts be current?

State the source, date, identity scope, document types, and method.

Should confidential reviews be exposed?

No. Use authorized bounded evidence and protect confidential material.

Does under review mean accepted?

No. Preserve the exact publication status.

What if evidence changes after appointment?

Reopen the register and use the approved correction and governance route.

Worked case: coordinate a public executive transition

Trigger: A public-company chief executive is leaving after a merger while a nonpublic restructuring, active employment dispute, disclosure timetable, confidential board material, stale public biographies, company-system access, competing accounts of performance, and a future board search overlap.

Can the resume describe the restructuring before it is announced?
No. Establish board, counsel, disclosure, confidentiality, and public-record authority first.
Which performance account should the bio use?
Reconcile metrics, attribution, filings, and disputes, then use only approved supportable wording.
Should references receive a suggested script?
Provide verified scope and boundaries without asking anyone to give false endorsement.
What proves closeout?
Approved versions, timed release, recipient records, revoked access, returned data, corrections, and monitoring.

Failure rehearsal

A recruiter publishes an outdated biography before the authorized filing. The team preserves the page, limits further distribution, coordinates the correction through counsel and communications, verifies no restricted fact was added, and updates the controlled source package.

Questions people actually ask

Who approves departure wording?

The authorities defined by governance, contract, disclosure, legal, and communications requirements.

Can a disputed metric stay in the resume?

Only if approved evidence supports accurate bounded wording; otherwise freeze it.

May company documents enter a personal portfolio?

Only with explicit authority and after records, privacy, confidentiality, and security review.

Should every setback be public?

Follow disclosure duties and truth; do not speculate, conceal required facts, or volunteer protected detail.

When is the package final?

After release, account closure, data return, correction monitoring, and retention are verified.

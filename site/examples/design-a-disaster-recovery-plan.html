Worked case: recover a regional order service

Trigger: A regional outage and corrupted replica could remove our primary service and newest customer data at the same time.

Design disaster recovery for our order API and database. We say recovery takes one hour.
I need the critical journeys, evidence behind that hour, acceptable data loss, failure domains, and who can authorize failover.
Order capture is critical. The one-hour claim has never been tested. We believe backups run every fifteen minutes.
Then one hour and fifteen minutes are hypotheses. I will separate the recovery time objective from the recovery point objective and design exercises to measure both.
Can the replica take traffic immediately?
Not until corruption isolation, write authority, secrets, queues, and reconciliation are proven. Otherwise failover could spread the failure.

Required evidence

  • Recovery architecture: failure domains, protected backups, identity, network, data authority, restoration order, and degraded modes.
  • Tabletop record: activation decision, missing authority, customer communication, and corrective owners.
  • Recovery rehearsal: measured failover, restore, reconciliation, user journey, and return to normal operation.

Failure rehearsal

The exercise simulates loss of the primary region and suspected replica corruption. Operators freeze new writes, isolate the corrupted path, restore the latest verified point into the recovery region, replay accepted events, and compare order totals and identifiers. Traffic moves only after one test order and reconciliation pass. If write authority is ambiguous, the exercise stops rather than risking split-brain data.

Questions people actually ask

Is high availability the same as disaster recovery?

No. High availability handles expected component failure. Disaster recovery covers failures that exceed ordinary redundancy and require coordinated restoration.

Who chooses the recovery objectives?

Business and system owners choose them from tolerable user harm, data loss, obligations, cost, and measured capability.

Can a tabletop prove the plan works?

No. It proves roles and decisions. Component restores and production-shaped recovery exercises prove technical behavior.

When should failover stop?

Stop when isolation, write authority, data protection, access, or a safe return path cannot be verified.

What if the rehearsal misses the target?

Record the observed limit, keep the target unproven, assign corrective work, and repeat the exercise.

What if the review just tears my resume apart?

The bluntness is the kindness: you are about to send this page to strangers who will never explain their no. But every verdict arrives with its reason and its repair, made in the same sitting with your yes. Rewording a weak line uses only facts you supply; when a claim has no evidence behind it, the verdict is cut it or go build the evidence, never invent a number. What you end up with

Every line carrying a verdict, and every verdict applied to the file before you stop. Here is a slice of a review mid-session: four lines from one resume and what happened to each.

Specific, true, and a stranger can click the link. Untouched.

"Responsible for social media" → "Ran the robotics club Instagram: three posts a week, all graphics mine"
A real thing said weakly. The interview surfaced the facts; the reword states them.
NEEDS EVIDENCE 3
"Skills: Python, SQL, machine learning"
No other line on the page supports SQL or ML. Supply evidence now, or those two move to Cut.
CUT 4
"Objective: seeking a challenging position where I can leverage my skills..."  ·  "References available upon request"
Neither survives two minutes of interview questioning. Both gone; the space goes to projects.
  1. Keep has a bar. Specific, true, and carrying a link or a checkable fact. Lines that clear it are left alone.
  2. Rewrites use only what happened. The skill asks what you actually did and rewords to the fact. Aspiration words like "passionate" become action verbs or die.
  3. Needs-evidence is the honest category. The gaps that survive the session go on a short list, and the fix for each is something to build. The skill will not paper over a gap with phrasing.
  4. Cuts free the six seconds. Objectives paragraphs, word-cloud skills, and middle-school entries spend the screener's attention on nothing. Removing them is the fastest improvement on most pages.

Questions people actually ask

Is this free?

The skill is free, no account, no catch. The agent you paste it into may or may not be: Gemini has the widest free tier, and the free plans of ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot can all run it in a chat.

Will it rewrite my resume for me?

It rewords weak phrasing using facts you confirm, with your yes on each change. What it will not do is invent: no added metrics, no upgraded titles, no skills you cannot defend. When a line needs evidence that does not exist, the verdict is to cut it or go build the evidence.

What does ATS-safe actually mean?

Applicant tracking systems parse your resume into a database before a human sees it. Multi-column layouts, graphics, and unusual fonts scramble that parsing, so your best line can arrive as garbage. The review flags these layout problems in the first pass and fixes them with you on the spot.

My resume is fine. Do I still need a review?

The six-second pass costs you nothing and tells you what a screener actually sees, which is regularly not what the writer sees. If every line earns a Keep, you walk away with a fresh PDF and confirmation. Most pages lose at least the objective paragraph.

How is this different from build-resume or tailor-resume?

Review works on a page that already exists and judges it line by line. Build starts from an interview when there is no page or the review gutted it. Tailor adapts a clean page to one specific posting. They chain: review first, then tailor for the job you want.

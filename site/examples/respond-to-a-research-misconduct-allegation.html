Worked case: protect people and test a disputed paper

Trigger: A multicenter medical paper is challenged after duplicated images, impossible timestamps, missing consent records, unexplained patient exclusions, inconsistent raw files, a disputed author contribution, and alleged retaliation against a junior reporter.

Should we ask the laboratory to investigate itself?
No. Protect people and participants, preserve evidence independently, and resolve conflicts before inquiry.
Do duplicated images prove fabrication?
They justify testing provenance and alternatives, but findings require the applicable evidence standard and fair response.
Can the paper stay in clinical use?
Use an authorized interim restriction or notice when continued reliance could cause harm.
What closes the case?
Independent findings, corrected research records, protected people, appeal, propagated action, and verified reform.

Protection is not a misconduct finding.

Failure rehearsal

An implicated administrator attempts to revoke the reporter’s access and edit shared files. Independent authority preserves immutable copies, blocks retaliation, records access, and continues a fair process without treating interference as proof of every allegation.

Questions people actually ask

Is an honest error misconduct?

Apply the governing definition and evidence standard rather than collapsing error and misconduct.

Can the reporter remain anonymous?

Protect identity to the extent lawful and feasible while explaining fair-process limits.

Who controls the evidence?

An independent authorized custodian preserves originals and logs access.

When should journals be told?

Use verified facts, applicable duties, and authorized interim or final notices.

Does unsubstantiated mean false?

No. It means the applicable standard was not met on the available evidence.

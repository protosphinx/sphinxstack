Worked case: replace a global checkout without losing money

Trigger: A global retailer must redesign checkout across guest and account users, multiple currencies, taxes, discounts, inventory holds, saved addresses, wallets, cards, subscriptions, accessibility needs, fraud controls, and regional legal terms while legacy and new flows overlap and duplicate orders or lost payments are unacceptable.

Can we compare conversion and send half of all users to the new flow?
Not yet. Define money and state invariants, exclusions, persistent assignment, stop conditions, and reconciliation first.
If payment times out, should the button invite another attempt?
No. Reconcile provider and ledger state before creating another financial attempt.
Can rollback route every session back to legacy checkout?
Only if it preserves accepted payments, orders, refunds, inventory holds, and webhook ownership.
What proves the redesign is safe?
Transaction reconciliation, critical accessibility journeys, bounded canaries, controlled recovery, and retirement evidence.

One customer intent must not become two charges.

Failure rehearsal

A wallet callback reaches the new flow after its session was routed back to legacy checkout. The shared intent identifier prevents a second attempt, the transaction owner reconciles the provider result, the customer receives one clear order status, and the affected cohort is removed until the callback handoff is fixed.

Questions people actually ask

Is conversion the primary release gate?

No. Financial correctness, accessibility, customer protection, and recovery are release gates.

Can both flows write the same order?

No. Define one authoritative writer for each state transition and enforce idempotency.

How should ambiguous payments be handled?

Pause retries and reconcile the provider, ledger, order, and customer outcome.

What does accessible checkout testing cover?

Complete purchase and recovery journeys across keyboard, screen reader, zoom, errors, challenges, and payment methods.

When can legacy checkout retire?

After outstanding sessions, webhooks, refunds, subscriptions, support tools, and retained records have verified dispositions.

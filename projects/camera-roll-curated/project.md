## Brief

You already shoot your friends' games and events. Pick your twenty
best shots, put them on a page, and use the page to offer shooting
the next event on purpose. The hard part is the cut from hundreds to
twenty; the offer rides on how ruthless the cut is.

## Personalize

- **The twenty.** From your own camera roll, cut hard. Twenty is a
  ceiling. If shot fifteen is weaker than shot five, the page is
  better at fifteen.
- **The consent.** Everyone recognizable in a published shot said
  yes. For teammates, ask in the team chat and keep the answers.
- **The offer.** The next game, recital, or birthday you could shoot
  deliberately. Free or a small price, with delivery promised as a
  shared album within a stated number of days.
- **The client.** One team, club, or family with an event coming up.
  Name them in your plan before you build.

## Milestones

1. Longlist of sixty pulled from the camera roll, cut to twenty.
2. Consent collected for every recognizable person; refusals cut
   without argument.
3. Page live by mid-week: the twenty, the offer, the contact.
4. Offer sent to the one team, club, or family you picked.
5. The event shot and the album delivered on the promised day.
6. Final README: what you shot, what you delivered, what the next
   twenty should look like.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; images compressed enough to load fast on a
  phone
- Every recognizable person consented; anyone who declined does not
  appear
- Honest-claims check passed: every photo on the page was shot by you
- First offer sent and the response logged in SESSIONS.md, which has
  an entry per session
- If the shoot was paid, the price was agreed before the event and
  handled by you in person

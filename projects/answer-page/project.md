## Brief

There is a question your friends keep bringing to you: which phone
to buy, how to fix their free kick, what to watch next, how to get
the game running on a bad laptop. Answer it once, completely, at a
URL you can send instead of retyping. One question, one page, depth
over coverage. The next time someone asks and you reply with the
link, the project has paid off.

## Personalize

- **The question.** Scroll your chats and count. The question you
  have answered most in the last three months is the one, whether
  or not it sounds impressive.
- **Your track record.** Why do people ask you? Put the evidence on
  the page: the setups you have fixed, the picks that landed.
- **The askers.** The three people most likely to ask next are your
  reviewers. Their follow-up questions are your missing sections.

## Milestones

1. Your usual chat-length answer live at a URL, day one.
2. The complete answer written: the short version on top, the full
   reasoning below, the cases where your advice changes.
3. Whatever the answer needs to be followable: photos, a comparison
   table, numbered steps.
4. The three askers read it; every follow-up question they still
   have becomes a new section.
5. Phone styling, since it will be opened from a chat message.
6. Send the link the next time the question comes in, and note what
   happened. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable from a chat-opened phone browser
- The answer is complete enough that no follow-up went unanswered
  in review
- Reviewer questions and the sections they caused, in the README
- The link has been sent in reply to the question at least once
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

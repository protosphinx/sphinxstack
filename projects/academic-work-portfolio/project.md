## Brief

Choose four pieces of academic work that show what you can do, not
just the grades you received. Publish each with its original context,
your specific contribution, one piece of process evidence, and an
honest reflection on what changed. Team projects must name the team
and separate your work. Restricted course material, participant data,
and other people's work stay private or are replaced with approved
excerpts.

## Personalize

- **The audience.** Pick one real next step: a program application,
  research role, internship, or adviser review.
- **The evidence mix.** Show more than polished finals: a revision,
  method note, critique response, test, or process artifact.
- **The permissions.** Ask collaborators and instructors what may be
  shared before publishing.

## Milestones

1. Audience criteria mapped to an inventory of possible work.
2. Four artifacts selected for relevance, range, and progression.
3. A contribution and permission record completed for every item.
4. Four case pages written with context, evidence, result, and reflection.
5. Accessible site published; files, links, captions, and mobile layout tested.
6. A teacher, adviser, or peer reviews what the portfolio proves;
   their interpretation and the resulting revisions are recorded.

## Done means

- Live portfolio contains exactly four focused academic cases
- Every claim and artifact has verified authorship and context
- Team contribution, permissions, and redactions are explicit
- Links, files, captions, reading order, and phone layout pass checks
- Independent feedback and the final revision are recorded

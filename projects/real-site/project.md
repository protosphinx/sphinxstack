## Brief

Three pages, mobile-first, deployed: a site for a subject that exists:
an actual club, team, family business, event, or person (you count).
Real content only: real names, real photos you have rights to, real
dates. No lorem ipsum, no fictional pizza shop. The site is finished
when the subject would actually share the link.

## Personalize

- **The subject.** Something you can get real content for this week.
  A family business beats a fictional startup every time.
- **The three pages.** Home plus the two pages the subject actually
  needs: schedule? menu? about? You decide from what a visitor wants.
- **Who signs off.** If the subject is not you, name the person who
  will look at it and say "yes, share it."

## Milestones

1. One ugly page live at a URL, day one.
2. Real content gathered and in the repo: text and images, rights
   confirmed.
3. All three pages structured, navigable, deployed.
4. Mobile-first styling; readable on a phone.
5. Sign-off from the subject; their feedback handled.
6. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; works on a phone screen
- Three pages, all real content, navigation between them
- Subject's sign-off recorded in the README
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

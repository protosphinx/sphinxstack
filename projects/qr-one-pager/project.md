## Brief

Your club gets a table at the fair, and the table gets a QR code.
This is the page the code opens: one screen that says what the club
does, when it meets, and how to join. It has to work on a stranger's
phone in the three seconds before they lose interest, and it has to
be live before the fair starts.

## Personalize

- **The club and the fair.** A real club with a real tabling date:
  the school activities fair, an open day, a community event. The
  deadline is fixed, which is the point.
- **The sponsor's sign-off.** The club sponsor or president approves
  the page and the QR code before either is printed. Build it with
  the club so members recognize it as theirs.
- **The one action.** Decide what a visitor should do: fill the
  interest form, join the chat, show up Thursday. One action, made
  obvious. Everything else on the page supports it.

## Milestones

1. Agree the content and the one action with the club's leadership.
2. One screen live at a URL: what, when, where, how to join. No
   scrolling required for the essentials.
3. The join action wired up and tested end to end from a phone.
4. QR code generated, test-scanned from three different phones,
   printed on the table sign or flyer.
5. Sponsor and president sign off; page is live before fair day.
6. Fair day: the code gets scanned by visitors. Afterward, record
   how many joined or signed up. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; essentials visible without scrolling on a
  phone
- Sign-off from the sponsor or president recorded in the README
- The QR code was printed, displayed at the fair, and scanned by
  people outside the club
- At least one person joined or signed up through the page, noted in
  the README
- Any photo on the page was taken with the consent of the people in
  it
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by
  you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

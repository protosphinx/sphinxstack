## Brief

Transcribe a grandparent's letters, postcards, or diary into pages you
can search and read, with the scanned originals alongside each
transcription. Permission comes first: it is their story, and they
decide what goes in and what stays private.

## Personalize

- **Their yes, in advance.** Ask before scanning anything, show them
  the site before it goes anywhere, and honor every cut they request.
  A private, password-shared version is a fine outcome.
- **The collection you can reach.** One shoebox is enough. Twenty
  transcribed letters with scans beat two hundred loose scans.
- **Their voice, preserved.** Transcribe exactly, spelling quirks and
  all. Your notes and explanations go in a visibly separate spot on
  the page.

## Milestones

1. Get permission and agree on the ground rules: what is in, what is
   out, who can see it.
2. Scan or photograph every item; name the files by date so the set
   sorts itself.
3. Transcribe each item into its own page, original image alongside;
   mark unreadable words as unreadable rather than guessing.
4. Add structure: an index ordered by date, and tags for people and
   places mentioned.
5. Read the whole archive through with the owner; make their
   corrections and cuts.
6. Publish (or share privately) and give them the first link.

## Done means

- Every published item has both scan and transcription
- The owner reviewed the site and approved what appears
- Transcriptions are faithful; your commentary is visibly separate
- Index and tags make any letter findable without scrolling everything
- Unreadable or uncertain words are marked, never invented
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

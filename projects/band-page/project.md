## Brief

A site for your band, or your friend's band: who plays what, one
recording a visitor can press play on, upcoming gigs even if the
next gig is a garage, and photos from practice. Bands need a link to
send people, and "we're on streaming eventually" is not a link. One
rough live recording on a page beats a bio with no sound.

## Personalize

- **The band.** Yours or one you know well enough to get files from.
  If it is not your band, the members approve everything before it
  ships.
- **The recording.** One track, recorded on a phone at practice if
  that is what exists. Get it as a file into the repo, playable in
  the browser.
- **The photos.** From a rehearsal or gig that happened, of these
  members, in the space they play in.

## Milestones

1. Band name and member list live at a URL, day one.
2. The recording playing in the browser with a native audio element.
3. Photos gathered and picked; a gigs section listing whatever is
   next, however small.
4. Styling that fits the band's sound, decided with the band.
5. Band review; corrections handled.
6. The link posted where the band promotes itself. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; audio plays on a phone
- One recording by this band, playable on the page
- Photos from a rehearsal or gig that happened, with member consent
- Band sign-off recorded in the README
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

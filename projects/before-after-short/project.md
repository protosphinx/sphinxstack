## Brief

A thirty-second cut of a transformation you did with your hands: the
room you reorganized, the bike you restored, the garden bed you dug.
Shot as you go, which means the before footage has to exist before
you start the work. You do the work and the edit; the agent helps
plan coverage and critiques cuts.

## Personalize

- **The transformation.** One you were going to do anyway this week,
  finishable in days. A half-done garage is footage without an
  ending.
- **The fixed frame.** Pick one camera position for the before and
  after shots and mark it with tape. The match cut is the whole
  payoff.
- **The middle.** A few working clips: hands, mess, progress. Enough
  to prove you did it yourself.

## Milestones

1. A numbered shot list covers the fixed before/after frame, working
   details, transitions, and one fallback; then the before footage is
   shot from the marked position.
2. The work done, with working clips grabbed along the way.
3. After footage shot from the same marked position, same time of
   day if the light matters.
4. Cut to thirty seconds, ending on the match cut.
5. Caption written by you, then published.

## Done means

- Live at a public URL, thirty seconds or close to it
- Before and after share the same framing; the change is undeniable
- The work in the middle is yours, on camera
- Shot dates are honest; nothing was staged to look worse before
- The caption says what it took: hours, days, cost if relevant

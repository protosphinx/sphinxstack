## Brief

Everything you and your friends are finished with, listed with
condition, price, and photos, aimed at next year's students at your
school. The list is the catalog; the deals happen face to face at
school, cash in hand, which keeps the whole thing scam-proof by
default.

## Personalize

- **The books.** Yours and your friends'. Each friend prices their
  own books and receives their own money; you run the list.
- **Condition honesty.** A photo of the worst page, highlighting and
  missing covers disclosed. A buyer who feels tricked tells the whole
  year group.
- **The buyers.** The class below you. Share through the channels
  they use, and the school noticeboard with permission.
- **The handoff.** At school, in daylight, cash preferred. If a
  payment app is used, the seller confirms the money inside the app
  before the book changes hands, since screenshots are faked.

## Milestones

1. List live on day one with your own books.
2. Friends' books added by mid-week, each priced by its owner.
3. Link shared with next year's students through their class group
   and the noticeboard.
4. First sale done at school; the book marked sold the same day.
5. Final README: what sold, what did not, and the prices that worked.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; every listing has condition photos
- Honest-claims check passed: condition descriptions match the
  photos, damage disclosed
- Each seller receives their own money, handed over in person
- First sale or inquiry logged in SESSIONS.md, which has an entry per
  session
- Sold items marked sold within a day
- If anything is also listed on a public marketplace, you can name
  the common scams before replying to buyers: overpayment refund
  requests, fake payment-app screenshots, courier-collection stories

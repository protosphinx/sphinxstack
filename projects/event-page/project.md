## Brief

Some event is coming that people keep texting the same questions
about: your cousin's wedding weekend, the school fest, the campout.
Build the one link that answers all of it: times, addresses, what to
wear, what to bring. From then on you reply with the URL instead of
retyping the answer.

## Personalize

- **The event.** A real event with a date soon enough to matter and
  far enough away to build for. You should be close to whoever is
  organizing it.
- **The organizer's blessing.** If the event is not yours, the
  organizer approves the page and its contents before the link goes
  anywhere. Their word beats your formatting.
- **The questions you already got.** Read back through your texts.
  Every question someone sent you about this event becomes a section
  on the page.

## Milestones

1. Collect the facts from the organizer and list the questions
   people have already asked.
2. One page live at a URL with the essentials: what, when, where.
3. Full schedule and addresses in, each address opening in a map
   app.
4. The judgment calls added, confirmed with the organizer: dress,
   gifts, parking, weather plan.
5. Organizer signs off; the link goes out in the invite thread or
   group chat.
6. Questions that still arrive get answered on the page, and the
   asker gets the link. Final README after the event, with one line
   on how it went.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Organizer sign-off recorded in the README before the link went out
- The link was sent to actual attendees and used to answer at least
  one incoming question
- Every time and address on the page was confirmed with the
  organizer
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by
  you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

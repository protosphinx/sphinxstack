## Brief

A page parents can forward to other parents: your availability this
month, your rate, and short quotes from families you have sat for,
used with their permission. Nothing goes on it that you could not back
up if a parent called to check. New work comes from the families you
already sit for passing the link along.

## Personalize

- **The references.** The families you already sit for. Ask each for
  one line and written permission to use it with their first name.
- **The rate.** Ask the parents you sit for what the going rate is
  where you live, and charge that. Say if the rate changes after
  midnight or with more kids.
- **What you say about yourself.** Age, school year, and any course
  you have completed (first aid only if you hold the certificate).
  No home address, no daily schedule.
- **The availability.** True this month, and kept current. A page
  with stale availability wastes a parent's evening.

## Milestones

1. Page live on day one: rate, availability, contact your parents
   okayed.
2. Quotes collected from at least two families, permission recorded.
3. Page sent by mid-week to the families you sit for, asking them to
   forward it to one other family.
4. Availability updated after every booking.
5. First inquiry from a new family answered and logged.
6. Final README: what parents asked before booking, and what on the
   page did the convincing.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Every quote has the family's recorded permission
- Honest-claims check passed: certificates and experience listed are
  ones you hold, the rate is what you charge
- No home address or school schedule anywhere on the page
- First new-family inquiry logged in SESSIONS.md, which has an entry
  per session
- Parents pay you directly, in person or by an app your parents
  okayed; the page never handles money

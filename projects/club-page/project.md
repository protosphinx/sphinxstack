## Brief

A page for a club you are in: what it does, when and where it meets,
how to join, and a photo from a meeting that happened. The difference
from a practice project is the approval step: the sponsor or club
leader reviews it, and the URL goes on the next flyer or announcement.
You are building for an organization with a stake in how it looks.

## Personalize

- **The club.** One you attend, so the content comes from firsthand
  knowledge and you can take the photo yourself.
- **The approver.** Name the sponsor, teacher, or president who has
  to say yes before the link goes anywhere. Tell them the plan
  before you build.
- **The join step.** Find out exactly how a new person joins (show
  up? talk to someone? sign a form?) and write that, since it is the
  page's whole job.

## Milestones

1. A one-paragraph page live at a URL, day one.
2. Content confirmed with the club: meeting time, place, activities,
   the join process.
3. A photo taken at a meeting, with everyone in it okay with being
   on the page.
4. Full page built and styled, readable on a phone.
5. Approver review; every requested change handled.
6. The URL published somewhere official: flyer, morning
   announcement, group chat pin. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; works on a phone
- Meeting details confirmed correct by a second club member
- A meeting photo with consent from the people in it
- Approver sign-off and the official posting recorded in the README
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

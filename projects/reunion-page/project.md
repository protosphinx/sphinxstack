## Brief

One page for the reunion your family is planning: date, place,
directions, who has confirmed, what each household is bringing. The
page replaces the twenty-message threads where this information keeps
getting lost. It goes in the group chat and gets checked from there
until the day of the event.

## Personalize

- **The reunion.** A real family gathering with a date within reach:
  a reunion, a big birthday, a holiday everyone travels for. If
  nothing is planned, this project is what gets one planned.
- **The organizer.** Someone is already herding this event, probably
  an aunt or a grandparent. They are your client. They approve the
  page before it goes to the chat, and their corrections win.
- **What people keep asking.** Listen to the group chat for a week.
  The questions that repeat are your sections.

## Milestones

1. Sit with the organizer and collect the facts: date, address, start
   time, parking, what still needs deciding.
2. One page live at a URL with those facts, checked by the organizer.
3. Confirmed list and bring list added, updated by you as replies
   come in through the chat.
4. Directions section done properly: address that opens in a map app,
   plus the one instruction maps get wrong for that place.
5. Link posted in the family group chat by the organizer, and you
   update the page as questions come in.
6. After the event, a photo and a line about how it went. Final
   README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Organizer's sign-off recorded in the README before the link went out
- The link was posted in the family chat and the page answered at
  least one question that would otherwise have been re-asked
- Names appear only for people who said yes to being listed
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

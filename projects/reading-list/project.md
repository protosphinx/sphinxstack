## Brief

Every book you have finished, listed publicly, with two honest lines
about each. The rule that makes the site worth reading: no book goes
up unfinished, and no opinion goes up that you would not say out
loud. This is a maintained site, so the build includes proving you
will update it: at least one book has to be added after launch.

## Personalize

- **Your backlog.** List every book you can honestly claim to have
  finished, going back as far as memory serves. School-assigned
  books count and are often where the honest lines get interesting.
- **Your two-line format.** Decide what your two lines do: verdict
  plus who should read it, or what it is plus what it did to you.
  Same format every book.
- **The current book.** Whatever you are reading now goes on the
  site as "in progress" and becomes your first post-launch entry.

## Milestones

1. Five books live at a URL, day one, plain list.
2. The full backlog entered with dates finished, as near as you can
   reconstruct.
3. Two honest lines written for every book, in your fixed format.
4. Sorting or grouping that fits how you think about them: by year,
   by verdict, by genre.
5. Phone styling; the list should be pleasant to scroll.
6. Finish the current book and add it, which proves the update path
   works. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; scrolls well on a phone
- Every finished book listed; nothing padded, nothing unfinished
- Two lines per book in a consistent format, written by you
- At least one entry added after the site first went live
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

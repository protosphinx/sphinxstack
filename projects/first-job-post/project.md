## Brief

A post about your part-time job that could only have been written by
someone who worked it: the customer stories, the boredom, the thing
your manager said that stuck. Specific beats profound. You write it;
the agent workshops drafts by asking where the details are thin.
Published where a stranger, or a future employer, could read it.

## Personalize

- **The job.** The one you have or had, with its own smells and
  regulars. If you have not had a job yet, this idea waits until you
  have.
- **The moments.** Pick the scenes you already retell to friends.
  Build the post around those instead of around lessons.
- **What you leave out.** Coworkers and customers did not sign up to
  be written about. Change names and skip what would embarrass
  someone.

## Milestones

1. A memory dump written fast: every scene, quote, and detail you can
   recall from the job.
2. The angle chosen: the one thread that ties your best scenes
   together.
3. Full draft written by you.
4. Workshop pass: the agent flags vague spots, and you replace them
   with specifics only you know.
5. A check for anyone who could be hurt by a detail, then published
   at a public URL.

## Done means

- Live at a public URL with your name on it
- The details are specific enough that nobody else could have written
  it
- Every sentence is yours; the agent only questioned drafts
- No coworker or customer is identifiable without their okay
- Everything described happened, told straight

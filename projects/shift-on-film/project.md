## Brief

A day-in-the-life short of your part-time job: opening, the rush, the
lull, closing. Shot on your phone with your manager's permission and
edited to show what the work is like, boredom included. You film and
cut it; the agent helps with the shot plan and reviews edits. Honest
footage of an ordinary shift holds up better than a highlight reel.

## Personalize

- **The job.** The one you clock into. If cameras are banned on the
  floor, film your prep, your commute, and the close instead.
- **The permission.** Your manager's okay first, in writing if the
  chain is big. Coworkers and customers stay out of frame unless
  they agree.
- **The through-line.** One shift from clock-in to clock-out is the
  simplest cut that works.

## Milestones

1. Manager's permission secured; the no-film zones written down.
2. Shot plan for one shift: ten short clips you can grab between
   tasks.
3. The shift filmed, without letting the filming eat the shift.
4. Cut to under two minutes, with timestamps or captions marking the
   hours.
5. A pass for faces and screens that should not appear, then
   published.

## Done means

- Live at a public URL
- Your manager knew and approved, before filming and before posting
- No coworker or customer appears without agreeing to it
- The shift shown is a normal one; nothing staged for drama
- Captions or narration, if any, are your own words

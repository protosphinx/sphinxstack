## Brief

One recorded conversation with someone who holds your neighborhood's
memory: the shop owner, the crossing guard, the neighbor who has been
here forty years. Edited with care into a listenable episode. You ask
the questions and make the cuts; the agent helps you prepare and plan
the edit. This is someone else's story in their voice, so their
consent runs through every step.

## Personalize

- **The person.** Someone you already greet. Knocking cold on a
  stranger's door is a different and harder project.
- **What only they know.** What the corner looked like before the
  building, who ran the old shop, what changed and when. Write your
  questions from curiosity about your own street.
- **The setting.** Their shop, their porch, wherever they are at
  ease. Street noise in the background can stay; it is the setting.

## Milestones

1. The person asked, the project explained, recording and publishing
   both agreed to.
2. Questions drafted by you, workshopped, cut to a page.
3. The conversation recorded where they are comfortable.
4. Edited into an episode, with a short recorded intro by you naming
   who they are and where this is.
5. They hear the cut and approve it, then published at a URL or
   feed.

## Done means

- Live at a public URL or feed
- The person consented on tape or in writing, and heard the episode
  before release
- Their voice carries the episode; your edits serve their story
- Names, dates, and places stay as they said them
- The episode notes, in your words, say who this is and why you
  asked

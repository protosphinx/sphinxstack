## Brief

Every book you finished this year, reviewed in two paragraphs each:
the assigned ones you hated, the embarrassing one you loved, all of
them. No plot summaries pasted from anywhere; your reaction is the
content. You write every review; the agent challenges lazy verdicts
and pushes for specifics. Books you abandoned get a one-line entry
saying so.

## Personalize

- **Your year's list.** Whatever you finished, even if it is four
  books. Four honest reviews beat twenty invented ones.
- **Your verdicts.** Disagreeing with the consensus is allowed and
  interesting. So is liking the assigned book.
- **A recurring line.** One question you answer for every book
  (would you reread it? who should skip it?) gives the page a spine.

## Milestones

1. The list assembled from memory, shelves, and school reading, with
   finished and abandoned marked.
2. Reviews drafted by you, two paragraphs each, starting with the
   books you remember most sharply.
3. Workshop pass: the agent flags any review that could have been
   written without reading the book; you sharpen those.
4. Page assembled in reading order or ranked, your call.
5. Published at a public URL.

## Done means

- Live at a public URL
- Every finished book of the year appears; abandonments are labeled
- Each review contains at least one specific only a reader would
  know
- Every word is yours; no borrowed summaries
- The embarrassing favorite made the page

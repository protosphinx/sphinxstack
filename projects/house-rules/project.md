## Brief

Your game group argues about the same rules every single time:
whether that card stacks, what landing on Free Parking pays, whether
screen-watching is cheating. Write the disputed rules down, put each
one to a vote, and publish the ratified set. The next dispute gets
settled by opening the URL instead of relitigating.

## Personalize

- **The group and the games.** Your actual game group and the games
  you play together. List the disputes from memory; the group will
  happily remind you of the rest.
- **The ratification process.** Agree how a rule passes: majority,
  unanimous, host decides ties. Write the process on the site too,
  because the group will eventually argue about the process.
- **The rule format.** Each rule gets the ruling, the date it
  passed, and the vote count. Whether you record who voted which way
  is up to the group; ask before attributing votes to names.

## Milestones

1. Collect the disputed rules from the group and agree the voting
   process.
2. Site live at a URL with the process and the first drafted rules
   marked as unratified.
3. Ratification round one: the group votes, results recorded, rules
   marked as passed with dates.
4. The full set ratified and published, organized by game.
5. Game night happens; a dispute comes up; someone opens the page
   and the ruling stands. This is the acceptance test.
6. Amendment path proven: one rule gets revisited, revoted, and the
   page shows the change with its date. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; every ratified rule carries its date and vote
  result
- The voting process is published and was followed
- A live dispute at a game night was settled by the page, noted in
  the README with the rule involved
- Named vote records appear only with each voter's okay
- At least one amendment went through the process after initial
  publication
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by
  you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

## Brief

A well-organized reference for something you already know deeply: a
game, a sport, a music scene, a hobby. Depth of content is the
feature: at least ten pages, or one serious data-driven page. The test
is whether another fan would bookmark it.

## Personalize

- **The topic.** The thing your friends already ask you about. If you
  can talk about it for ten minutes without preparation, it qualifies.
- **The shape.** Ten linked pages (guides, entries, tier lists) or one
  deep data page (a sortable table of every item/character/route)?
  Pick based on how your knowledge is actually structured.
- **The organizing scheme.** Alphabetical, by category, by difficulty,
  by era? Defend the choice in the README: organization is the craft
  being judged here.

## Milestones

1. Content inventory in the README: every page or table column you
   will build, before any code.
2. Skeleton live: navigation and empty pages deployed.
3. Half the content in, real and specific.
4. All content in; internal links between related entries.
5. Phone-readable styling; a fan can find any entry in two clicks.
   Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; works on a phone screen
- Ten-plus pages or one data page with 30-plus real entries
- Navigation reaches everything; no dead links
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

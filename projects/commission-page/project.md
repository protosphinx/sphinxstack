## Brief

A page for the thing you already make, whether that is drawings,
baked goods, or bracelets: photos of finished pieces, what a request
costs, and how to ask for one. Nothing goes on the offer list until
you have made it at least once and timed yourself, so every promise
on the page has been tested.

## Personalize

- **The craft.** The one you already do unprompted. The page shows
  work that exists, so if your camera roll has nothing to show, make
  three pieces first.
- **The offer.** The sizes or variants you have tested, with a
  turnaround based on how long the test piece took, padded for
  homework weeks.
- **The price.** Materials plus your time at a rate you can say out
  loud without flinching. Write the math in the README.
- **How to ask.** A form or DM your parents okayed. Payment on
  delivery, in person.

## Milestones

1. Page live on day one with three finished pieces photographed.
2. One commission-sized test piece made and timed; price computed
   from materials and hours.
3. Offer listed by mid-week with price and honest turnaround.
4. Page shared with the people who have already asked you for one.
5. First request handled start to finish and logged: the ask, the
   making, the handoff.
6. Track inquiries through contacted, scoped, accepted, declined,
   making, and delivered with a dated next action for every open request.
7. Final README with the pricing math and what the first request
   taught you.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Honest-claims check passed: every offered item has been made at
  least once, every photo is your own work
- Price covers materials, with the math written in the README
- First request or inquiry logged in SESSIONS.md, which has an entry
  per session
- Buyer pays you directly on delivery; the page never collects money

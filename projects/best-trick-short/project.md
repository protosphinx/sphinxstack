## Brief

A sixty-second video of the one skill people ask you to show them:
the kickflip, the omelette flip, the speedcube solve, the free-throw
streak. Shot on your phone and cut tight so every second earns its
place. You perform it and you make the cuts; the agent helps plan
shots and critiques drafts of the edit, but everything in the frame
happened in front of your camera.

## Personalize

- **The trick.** One you can land on camera this week, most attempts.
  If it takes fifty tries, film fifty tries; the fails can be the
  opening.
- **The location and light.** Somewhere you already do this thing.
  Daylight beats your ceiling bulb.
- **The angle that sells it.** Every trick has one view that makes it
  read. Find yours before the shoot day, by testing.

## Milestones

1. Shot list on paper: the angles, the close-up, the one slow-motion
   moment.
2. Footage shot, more takes than you think you need.
3. First cut at sixty seconds, no filler frames.
4. Sound decided and placed: room audio, a track you have rights to,
   or silence.
5. A caption written by you, then published where anyone can watch
   it.

## Done means

- Live at a public URL, sixty seconds or under
- Every frame is your footage, shot by you or a friend you credit
- No dead frames: pausing anywhere lands on something worth seeing
- Music, if any, is licensed or from the platform library
- The caption is in your voice

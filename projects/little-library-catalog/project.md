## Brief

The little free library on your corner has an inventory nobody can
see without walking over. Catalog it: what is on the shelf this
week, checked on your walk home, plus a shelf history of what came
and went. Neighbors check the page before walking over, and the
history becomes a small record of what your street reads.

## Personalize

- **The library.** A little free library you pass often enough to
  check weekly. If a household hosts it, tell them the plan and get
  their okay before publishing their box's location.
- **Your checking rhythm.** Pick the day and stick to it. The
  catalog is only useful if the "last checked" date is recent.
- **The shelf history.** Decide what a history entry looks like:
  arrived, taken, how long a book lasted. The book that vanished in
  a day says something; so does the one nobody will take.

## Milestones

1. First full inventory taken: every title and author on the shelf,
   photographed for your own reference.
2. Catalog live at a URL with the current shelf and a last-checked
   date.
3. Second week's check done; arrivals and departures recorded, and
   the shelf history page begins.
4. A photo of the library itself on the page, taken by you, with the
   host's okay if it sits on private property.
5. Four consecutive weekly checks completed; the history has real
   movement in it.
6. Card or note with the URL placed inside the library (host's okay
   first), so borrowers can find the page. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding, with a last-checked date under a week old at
  handoff
- Host household's okay recorded in the README, if the library has
  one
- Four weekly inventories done; shelf history shows arrivals and
  departures with dates
- The URL is posted at the library itself and at least one neighbor
  has seen the page, noted in the README
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by
  you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

## Brief

Store runs and pickups for the older neighbors who already know you,
with a page their adult children can read and trust: who you are,
what you do, and a contact form your parents okayed. The money
routine is spelled out on the page, because the routine is what earns
the trust.

## Personalize

- **The neighbors.** People who already know you or your family. This
  service does not go to strangers, and the page says who vouches for
  you.
- **The errands.** Store runs, package drop-offs, library returns.
  Prescription pickups only where the pharmacy allows a named third
  party, which the neighbor arranges with the pharmacy first.
- **The money routine.** The neighbor gives cash before the run; you
  bring back the receipt and exact change every time. You never hold
  anyone's card or PIN.
- **What the page shows.** Your name, your parents' awareness, your
  streets. No pay-me-online links of any kind.

## Milestones

1. Page live on day one: errand list, money routine, contact form.
2. Routine reviewed with your parents.
3. Page shared by mid-week with two or three neighbors who know you,
   and offered to their adult children if they want it.
4. First errand run: cash before, receipt and exact change after,
   logged.
5. Final README: runs made, what neighbors asked for most, how long a
   run takes.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Honest-claims check passed: the page says who you are and what you
  charge, and both are true
- Money routine followed on every run: cash before, receipt and exact
  change after, no card or PIN ever held
- First errand or inquiry logged in SESSIONS.md, which has an entry
  per session
- Prescriptions handled only through the pharmacy's own third-party
  pickup process
- Your parents know each neighbor you run for

## Brief

One newsletter issue that wraps your team's season for the people who
lived it: results, the moments, the bus story everyone already tells.
Written by you and sent to the players and parents. You gather the
material and do the writing; the agent helps organize and trim. It
reads like it came from inside the team, because it did.

## Personalize

- **The team.** Yours, this season. The recap only works for a season
  you spent on the roster or the bench.
- **The material.** Scores from the league page or your own records,
  photos from parents' phones with their okay, the moments you would
  retell anyway.
- **The tone call.** A losing season can still get a great recap.
  Decide whether this issue is funny, proud, or both, and hold that
  line all the way through.

## Milestones

1. The season's record assembled: every result in order, checked
   against someone else's memory or the league page.
2. The moments list, five to ten, gathered by asking teammates what
   they remember.
3. Full draft written by you.
4. Photos collected with permission, captioned, every name spelled
   right.
5. Sent to the team list and posted at a link the team can revisit.

## Done means

- Delivered to the team and viewable at a link
- Every score is correct and checkable
- Teammates in photos or quotes gave their okay
- The writing is yours, in your voice
- At least one teammate replies about a moment you included

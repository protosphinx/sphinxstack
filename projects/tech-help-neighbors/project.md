## Brief

Printers, wifi, phone settings, the TV remote situation. A page aimed
at the neighbors who already ask you, saying what you can fix, what
you will not touch, and how to reach you. The will-not-touch list is
what lets a neighbor's adult children read the page and relax.

## Personalize

- **The fix list.** Problems you have solved before: printer offline,
  wifi renamed, phone storage full, the TV input maze. Your own
  household counts as experience.
- **The will-not-touch list.** Passwords, banking, and buying things
  on anyone's account. You can sit next to a neighbor while they type
  their own password; you do not type it for them. Put the list on
  the page.
- **The neighbors.** The ones who already ask you or your parents.
  The page is written for them by name in your head.
- **The price.** Free, or a small flat visit price you set with your
  parents. Decide before the first visit, and say it on the page.

## Milestones

1. Page live on day one: fix list, will-not-touch list, contact.
2. Both lists reviewed with a parent.
3. Page shared by mid-week with the two or three neighbors who
   already ask.
4. First visit done and logged: what was broken, what fixed it, how
   long it took.
5. Turn recurring questions from the first inquiries into a checked
   customer FAQ, with the will-not-touch boundaries kept visible.
6. Final README that doubles as your cheat sheet of recurring fixes.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Honest-claims check passed: the fix list only contains problems you
  have solved
- Will-not-touch list on the page and followed on every visit: no
  passwords typed for anyone, no purchases made on anyone's account
- First visit or inquiry logged in SESSIONS.md, which has an entry
  per session
- Any payment agreed before the visit and handled in person

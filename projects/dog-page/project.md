## Brief

A page for the dog walking and sitting you already do: photos of the
dogs, what you charge, and which streets you cover. The owners you
already work for are your references; ask each for one line. New
owners find you because a current owner sent the link.

## Personalize

- **The dogs.** Only dogs you have walked or sat, photographed with
  the owner's permission. A page of stock dogs convinces nobody.
- **The services.** Walks, feeding visits, weekend sitting. List only
  what you have done before and what your parents allow.
- **The rates.** Per walk and per visit. Ask your current owners what
  is fair where you live.
- **The limits.** Sizes and behaviors you can handle. If you cannot
  hold an excited seventy-pound dog, the page should say so before an
  owner finds out on the sidewalk.

## Milestones

1. Page live on day one: services, streets, rates, contact.
2. Photos and one-line references gathered from current owners, with
   permission recorded.
3. Page shared by mid-week with your current owners, asking each to
   pass it to one dog household they know.
4. First new inquiry answered; meet the dog with its owner present
   before agreeing to anything.
5. Final README: which dogs, which streets, what you learned about
   scheduling around school.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Every photo and reference has the owner's recorded permission
- Honest-claims check passed: services listed are ones you have done,
  limits stated plainly
- First new-owner interaction logged in SESSIONS.md, which has an
  entry per session
- Owners pay you directly; the page has no payment link

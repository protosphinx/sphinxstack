## Brief

One trip you took, told day by day: your photos, what happened, and
the part that went wrong, because the flat tire or the missed train
is the story everyone retells. The photos already exist on your
phone; the work is picking the honest ones and writing what a photo
cannot show. Built for the people who were there and the you of ten
years from now.

## Personalize

- **The trip.** One that finished, that you have photos from, and
  that had at least one thing go sideways. A school trip or a
  weekend at a cousin's qualifies; glamour is irrelevant.
- **The other witnesses.** Whoever was on the trip gets asked for
  their photos and their version of the disputed moments.
- **The wrong turn.** Name the thing that went wrong before you
  start writing. It anchors the honest tone for everything else.

## Milestones

1. Day one of the trip live at a URL, day one of the build.
2. All photos collected from everyone who was there, culled to the
   ones that carry the story.
3. Every day written up, including the day things went wrong, told
   straight.
4. One page per day with navigation between days, plus an index.
5. Phone styling; trips get relived on phones.
6. Someone who was there reads it and disputes your version. Settle
   it in the text. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; works on a phone
- Every day of the trip covered with photos from the trip itself
- The thing that went wrong is in the story, told honestly
- A fellow traveler's review recorded in the README
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

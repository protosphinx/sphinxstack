## Brief

One page that says what yard work you do, which streets you cover,
what it costs, and how to text you. The same URL goes on a printed
flyer you deliver by hand to houses within walking distance. Money
changes hands in person, between you and the neighbor; the site never
takes a payment.

## Personalize

- **The jobs.** Only work you have done before with equipment you can
  use: mowing, raking, weeding, snow shoveling if it snows where you
  live. If you have never edged a lawn, edging does not go on the
  page.
- **The streets.** The ones you can walk to carrying a rake. Name
  them, so a neighbor two blocks over knows they count.
- **The prices.** Per job, set by asking one adult who pays for this
  kind of work what a fair price is. You will be the one sweating, so
  make sure the number is one you will still be happy with an hour in.
- **The contact.** A phone number or form your parents have okayed.

## Milestones

1. Page live at a URL on day one: jobs, streets, prices, contact.
2. Prices checked with an adult who has paid for yard work, then
   adjusted.
3. Flyer made with the URL and a QR code, printed at home or the
   library.
4. Flyers delivered by mid-week to at least 20 houses. Tape them to
   doors; in many places mailboxes are reserved for postal mail.
5. First inquiry answered the same day, and logged.
6. Final README: what people asked for, what got booked, what you
   would change.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Flyers delivered to at least 20 houses you can walk to
- Honest-claims check passed: every listed job is one you have done,
  every price is one you will work for
- First customer interaction logged in SESSIONS.md, even if it was
  only a question
- All money handled by you in person; the site never asks for payment
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

## Brief

One game your team played, written up like sports journalism: the
turning point, quotes collected on the sideline, the box score. You
attend or play, take notes, gather the quotes, and write the piece;
the agent workshops structure and pushes on flat spots. Published
where a teammate's parent could find it and relive the game.

## Personalize

- **The game.** A recent one you were at, ideally with something to
  say: an upset, a collapse, a debut. Any game works if you watched
  it closely.
- **The quotes.** Two or three people you can catch right after the
  final whistle: a teammate, the coach, the loudest parent. Write
  their words down exactly.
- **Playing or reporting.** If you are on the field, your notes come
  after the game; reconstruct it with a teammate before memories
  smooth over.

## Milestones

1. The game attended with a notebook: scoring plays, momentum
   shifts, times, the details a box score misses.
2. Quotes gathered from people at the game, with their okay to be
   named.
3. The box score compiled and checked against the league record or
   the scorekeeper.
4. The piece drafted by you: lede, turning point, quotes placed
   where they land.
5. Workshop pass, facts checked once more, then published at a
   public URL.

## Done means

- Live at a public URL
- The score and stats match the official or scorekeeper record
- Quotes are word for word and the speakers agreed to be named
- The writing is yours throughout
- Someone who missed the game can follow what happened and why it
  mattered

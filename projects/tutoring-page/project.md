## Brief

A page offering tutoring in the one subject you are genuinely good
at: your results as proof, what a session covers, and when you are
free. One subject only; depth is the whole pitch, and the page should
say no to everything else.

## Personalize

- **The subject.** The one classmates already text you about before
  tests. If nobody texts you about it, it is the wrong subject.
- **The proof.** A grade, a score, or a piece of work you can show.
  Every claim must survive a skeptical parent asking to see it.
- **The offer.** Session length, where it happens (library, school,
  video call), and the price or a free first session.
- **The students.** Classmates in the year below, or younger kids of
  family friends. Write the page for their parents too.

## Milestones

1. Page live on day one: subject, availability, contact.
2. Proof section written and checked: every result named is one you
   can show evidence for.
3. Page shared by mid-week with the classmates who already ask, and
   with one teacher who might refer students.
4. First session scheduled, or first inquiry answered and logged.
5. After the first session, one line from the student or their parent
   added with permission.
6. Final README: what the first student struggled with and how you
   would run session two.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- One subject on the page; availability current
- Honest-claims check passed: every result named has evidence you can
  produce
- First inquiry or session logged in SESSIONS.md, which has an entry
  per session
- Sessions happen in a public place or with a parent nearby
- Payment, if any, handled by you directly with the parent; the page
  never collects money

## Brief

The full biography of your pet: photos, habits, a life timeline, the
noises, the enemies, the spot on the couch. The subject is silly on
purpose; the execution is not. Because the content costs nothing to
gather, all your effort goes into making the site good, which is the
point. Finish it properly and you have proven you can finish a site.

## Personalize

- **The pet.** Yours, or a pet you see weekly. You need photo access
  and firsthand knowledge of its habits. A neighbor's cat counts if
  you know the cat.
- **The signature detail.** Every pet has one weird thing it does.
  That gets its own section, written with the seriousness of a
  museum placard.
- **The timeline events.** Gather dates from whoever remembers:
  arrival day, the great escape, the vet incident.

## Milestones

1. One page with one photo live at a URL, day one.
2. Photos gathered and picked: at least ten good ones spanning the
   pet's life so far.
3. The biography written: habits, timeline, likes and dislikes.
4. Multiple pages with navigation: home, timeline, gallery, and
   whatever else the pet has earned.
5. Styling pass until it looks deliberate on a phone and a laptop.
6. Show the household. If they laugh and share the link, ship the
   final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; works on a phone
- At least ten photos of the specific pet, laid out with care
- A timeline with dated events confirmed by the household
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

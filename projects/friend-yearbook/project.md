## Brief

A yearbook site for your friend group: photos, superlatives, and a
timeline of the year's moments, made to be looked at again in ten
years. Every person gets a page, and every person controls what is
on their own page. It ships while the year is still fresh enough
that people argue about the captions.

## Personalize

- **The group.** Your actual friends, by name, with the photos from
  your phones. Four people is enough; a whole class is too many for
  a week.
- **The superlatives.** Your group's own categories, voted on by the
  group. Skip anything that would sting; the test is whether the
  person laughing hardest at each award is the person who won it.
- **The timeline.** The moments your group already retells. If a
  story gets told at every hangout, it goes in.

## Milestones

1. Pitch the group and collect a yes from everyone who will appear.
   Anyone who declines is simply left out, without commentary.
2. One page live at a URL with the group name and one photo.
3. Photos gathered from everyone's phones, picked down to the good
   ones, each subject okay with each photo of them.
4. Person pages built; each friend reviews their own page and their
   superlative before it goes live.
5. Timeline page written and put in order.
6. Link sent to the group chat; corrections and protests handled.
   Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Every person who appears consented to their page, their photos,
  and their superlative, recorded in the README
- The link was shared in the group chat and the group has seen it
- Timeline covers the year with dates, in order
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by
  you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

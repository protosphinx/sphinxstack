## Brief

You and two strong friends, available weekends, for the
couch-up-the-stairs jobs in your own neighborhood. The page: who the
crew is, a photo, what you will move, what you charge, and how to
book. The refusal list keeps three teenagers from getting hurt, so it
goes on the page next to the prices.

## Personalize

- **The crew.** The two friends, named, with their parents in the
  loop from day one.
- **The job list.** What three people can carry without injury:
  couches, mattresses, dressers, boxed appliances with a dolly. The
  refusal list holds pianos, safes, and anything involving a ladder.
- **The price.** Flat per job or per hour, agreed before you show up,
  split evenly the same day.
- **The range.** Your own neighborhood, close enough that you arrive
  on foot.

## Milestones

1. Page live on day one: crew, job list, refusal list, price,
   booking contact.
2. Crew ground rules agreed and written down: how money splits, what
   happens on a no-show, who says no to an unsafe job.
3. Page shared by mid-week with neighbors and the local group your
   parents use.
4. First job done: doorways measured before lifting, lifting done
   with legs, nobody hurt.
5. Final README: jobs done, refusals made, what the crew changed
   after job one.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Honest-claims check passed: the photo is the crew, the job list is
  within what you have safely carried
- Refusal list on the page and enforced on every booking
- First booking or inquiry logged in SESSIONS.md, which has an entry
  per session
- Payment in cash or a parent-okayed app, split evenly the same day
- Every parent involved knows the time and address of every job

## Brief

The three routes you walk most, each on its own page with the
knowledge you have from walking it: the shortcut, the hill worth
avoiding, the house with the dog, the corner that floods after rain.
Photos taken by you on the route itself. No map service embed can
say what you know about these streets; that gap is the site.

## Personalize

- **The three routes.** Routes you walk often enough to know cold.
  School run, dog loop, the way to a friend's house.
- **What only a walker knows.** Each route page needs at least three
  details you could not get from a map: timing, terrain, hazards,
  the good bits.
- **The photo walk.** Schedule one walk per route with your phone
  out. Shots of the decision points, where a stranger would wonder
  which way to turn.

## Milestones

1. One route live at a URL, day one, text only and ugly.
2. All three routes walked with your phone out; photos in the repo.
3. Each route written up: turns, landmarks, warnings, rough time.
4. An index page with a one-line pitch for each route.
5. Phone styling, since the reader is standing on a corner.
6. One person walks a route using only your page and reports where
   they got confused. Fix it. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; usable on a phone mid-walk
- Three route pages, each with your own photos at decision points
- Test walk feedback and fixes recorded in the README
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

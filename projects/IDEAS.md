# 100 project ideas

These are starting points. Every one is meant to be bent around your actual life: your team, your family, your street, your job, your stuff. Pick one, then use the [start-a-project skill](https://sphinxstack.com/skills/start-a-project/) to turn it into a scoped project with milestones.

## Websites

- **Grandma's recipe archive** — Type up the handwritten family recipes before they fade, one page per dish, with photos of the original cards. Start with the one dish everyone requests. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Team schedule page** — A page for your team's game schedule that your teammates actually check. Dates, locations, results as they happen, updated by you every week. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Walking routes of your neighborhood** — The three routes you actually walk, with what's on each: the shortcut, the hill to avoid, the dog that barks. Photos you took yourself. _(skills: build-website)_
- **A page for your pet** — Photos, habits, a timeline, the full biography. Silly on purpose, finished for real. Your pet, not a generic pet. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Shoebox photo timeline** — Scan the box of old family photos nobody looks at, put them in order, and caption them by asking relatives who's who. _(skills: build-website)_
- **How-to for the thing you can actually do** — You can solve a cube, restring a guitar, fold a fitted sheet. Write the guide with your own photos at every step. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Your club's page** — What the club does, when it meets, how to join, one photo from a real meeting. Show it to the club sponsor before it goes on the flyer. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Band page** — For your band or your friend's, with one real recording, upcoming gigs even if the gig is a garage, and photos from actual practice. _(skills: build-website)_
- **A grandparent's life story** — Sit down with them, take notes, and build the page: where they grew up, what they did, the stories they always tell. They approve every word. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Glossary of your hobby's jargon** — Every term you had to learn the hard way for fishing, skating, knitting, or whatever yours is, defined the way you wish someone had defined it. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Your reading list, public** — Every book you finished, two honest lines each. The rule that makes it work: no book goes up unfinished. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The history of your street** — What was here before your house, from the library's local archive and the neighbor who's lived here forty years. _(skills: build-website)_
- **One trip, archived** — A real trip you took, day by day, with your photos and what actually happened, including the part that went wrong. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Your own start page** — The page your browser opens to: your links, your timetable, the weather widget you write yourself. Nobody else has to like it. _(skills: build-website, ship-on-github)_
- **Freshman survival guide** — What you wish someone had told you about your school: which stairwell, which lunch line, which teachers assign on Fridays. All from experience. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The question everyone asks you** — Friends always ask you the same thing: which phone to buy, how to fix their throw-in, what to watch next. Answer it once, completely, at a URL. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Desk setup page** — Your actual desk, every part of it listed: what it is, what it cost, why it's there, what you'd change. Update it when the setup changes. _(skills: build-website)_

## Apps & tools

- **Chore rotation board** — Whose turn is it, really. Your household's actual chores and actual people, with the rotation your family agreed to, settled on a screen instead of an argument. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Split-the-bill calculator** — Built for how your friend group actually splits: the one who only had fries, the one who forgot their wallet. Handles your real cases, not textbook ones. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Flashcards for one class** — The exact class you're studying for right now, with your own deck typed in from your own notes. Shuffle, flip, mark wrong ones for repeat. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Countdown board** — The real dates you care about: the exam, the season opener, your best friend's birthday, the license test. One screen, days remaining on each. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Dinner picker** — Loaded with the meals your family actually makes, nothing aspirational. One button, tonight's answer. Add a no-repeats-this-week rule. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Workout timer for your routine** — Your actual sets and rest intervals hardcoded first, editable second. Big numbers, loud beep, usable on a phone on the floor. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **The quote wall** — Funny things your friends and family have actually said, dated and attributed, with a random-quote button. Ask before you publish anyone. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Packing list generator** — For the trips you actually take: practice, camping, grandma's house. Pick the trip type, get your list, check things off. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Score keeper with house rules** — For the card or board game your family plays, including the weird rule nobody outside the house believes. Running totals across game nights. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Study timer with a log** — Your real subjects, a timer, and a plain log of what you did each session. The log is the point; the timer is the excuse. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Converter for your hobby** — Grams to cups for your baking, inches to millimeters for your builds, or the currency for the trip your family is planning. Only the conversions you use. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Decision spinner** — For the decisions your group actually repeats: who rides shotgun, who picks the movie, which chore is whose. Weighted however your group negotiates. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Paycheck calculator** — Your part-time job's actual pay rules: rate, weekend bump, the deductions on your real payslip. Enter hours, see what Friday brings. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Plant watering schedule** — The actual plants in your house, each with its own interval, and a today-view of what's thirsty. Bonus: the family stops killing the fern. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Which bus** — Your two real routes home, timetables typed in, and one answer given the current time: catch the 4:12 or wait for the 4:31. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Text adventure of your house or school** — Navigate your actual building room by room, with the jokes only people who've been there will get. Playtest it on a sibling. _(skills: build-web-app, ship-on-github)_
- **How well do you know me** — A quiz about you or your friend group with real questions and a score at the end. Send it to the group chat and watch them fail. _(skills: build-web-app)_

## Data

- **The season in numbers** — Every score from your team's season, hand-entered, with a results page and one chart: goals per game, win streaks, whatever your team argues about. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The free-stuff map** — Every water fountain, public bathroom, and free wifi spot near your school, each one verified on foot by you. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Price watch** — The one thing you're saving for, price checked daily across three stores for two weeks, charted, with the verdict on when and where to buy. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Family tree, rendered** — Interview your parents, build the tree as a plain data file, render it as a browsable page. Every date confirmed by someone who was there. _(skills: build-web-app, ship-on-github)_
- **The letters archive** — Transcribe a grandparent's letters, postcards, or diary into searchable pages, originals scanned alongside. Get permission first; it's their story. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The commute experiment** — Leave for school at a different time each day for a week, log every trip, chart it, and publish the answer to when you should actually leave. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Game-night standings** — Your friend group's real results across every game night, entered as they happen, with a standings table and each player's sorest statistic. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Paper to pixels** — Your club or team keeps a logbook, sign-in sheet, or scorebook on paper. Digitize it and give it a page. The keeper of the book signs off. _(skills: build-website, ship-on-github)_
- **The definitive local ranking** — Every taco, boba, or pizza place within walking distance of school, scored by you on the same fixed criteria, published as a table with dates visited. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Count something for a week** — Cafeteria line length every five minutes, hallway traffic between periods, cars running the stop sign on your corner. Hand-counted, charted, written up. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Your listening, charted** — Export your streaming history and chart what you actually played: top artists by month, the song you won't admit to, the genre drift. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Screen-time honesty page** — Your phone already has the numbers. Pull a week of them into a chart, write three sentences of commentary, and let the chart be embarrassing. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Forecast vs. reality** — Same spot, same time, every day: record the actual temperature at your window next to what the forecast promised. Chart the gap. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The missing stat table** — Your school or rec league doesn't publish the table you want: assists, saves, head-to-head. Build it by hand from game records and keep it current. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Sleep week** — A notebook by the bed, seven nights of real numbers, then a chart and one honest paragraph about what the data says versus what you tell people. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Pantry inventory** — Everything in the family freezer and pantry, entered once, with a page that shows what's oldest. Your household actually uses it or it doesn't count. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Street tree survey** — Every tree on your block, identified with a field guide, photographed, and mapped. Ask the neighbor with the garden to check your work. _(skills: build-website, ship-on-github)_

## Content

- **Interview a grandparent** — One recorded conversation about their life, edited into a listenable episode. The questions are yours; the stories are theirs; they hear it before anyone else. _(skills: podcast-episode)_
- **Write up the thing you fixed** — The bike, the lamp, the drowning group project. What broke, what you tried, what worked, with photos. Written so the next person can follow it. _(skills: blog-post)_
- **Your best trick, sixty seconds** — The skill you actually have, shot on your phone and edited tight: the kickflip, the omelette flip, the speedcube solve. No filler frames. _(skills: edit-a-short)_
- **Family newsletter, issue one** — What everyone's actually up to, gathered by texting your relatives, written up and sent to the family. If they ask when issue two is coming, it worked. _(skills: newsletter-issue)_
- **What my first job taught me** — The real version: the customer stories, the boredom, the thing your manager said that stuck. Specific enough that it could only be your job. _(skills: blog-post)_
- **Grandma cooks, you film** — One dish, start to finish, cut into a short with her hands doing the work and her narration over it. Pairs with the recipe archive if you built one. _(skills: edit-a-short)_
- **One episode with your best friend** — The topic you two already can't stop talking about, recorded on purpose for once, edited down to the good forty minutes. _(skills: podcast-episode)_
- **The season recap issue** — Your team's season written up for the parents and players who lived it: results, moments, the bus story everyone tells. Sent to the actual team. _(skills: newsletter-issue)_
- **Explain your niche to outsiders** — The hobby your family doesn't get, explained so they finally do. Written for one specific confused relative; you know which one. _(skills: blog-post)_
- **A shift at my job, on film** — A day-in-the-life short of your actual part-time job, shot with your manager's permission, edited honest rather than glamorous. _(skills: edit-a-short)_
- **The explainer classmates keep asking for** — You're the one people text before the test. Write the explanation once, properly, with the examples you always end up drawing anyway. _(skills: blog-post)_
- **An oral history from your street** — The shop owner, the crossing guard, the neighbor who remembers what the neighborhood used to be. One recorded conversation, edited with care. _(skills: podcast-episode)_
- **The club newsletter** — One issue for the members who missed the meetings: what was decided, what's next, who did what. Sent before the next meeting or it's history, not news. _(skills: newsletter-issue)_
- **Before and after, thirty seconds** — The room you reorganized, the bike you restored, the garden bed you dug. Shot as you go, cut to the transformation. _(skills: edit-a-short)_
- **Every book this year, reviewed honestly** — Including the assigned ones you hated and the embarrassing one you loved. Two paragraphs each, no summaries copied from anywhere. _(skills: blog-post)_
- **Cover your own team's game** — One real game written up like actual sports journalism: the turning point, the quotes you collected on the sideline, the box score. _(skills: blog-post)_
- **How your parents met** — Both of them, interviewed separately, then together. Edit the versions against each other. This one becomes a family heirloom. _(skills: podcast-episode)_

## Community & events

- **The family reunion page** — Date, place, directions, who's confirmed, what to bring. Built for the reunion your family is actually planning, linked from the group chat. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The answers page for your building or street** — Bin day, parking rules, who to call about the elevator. The stuff your street's group chat re-asks every month, pinned at a URL. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Tournament bracket** — Your friend group's actual tournament, whether it's Smash, FIFA, or ping pong in the garage. Bracket, results, a champion page for the winner's ego. _(skills: build-web-app)_
- **Friend-group yearbook** — Photos, superlatives, and the year's timeline for your actual group, with everyone's consent on their own page. Made to be looked at in ten years. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The minutes site** — Every decision your club has made, dated and searchable, so meetings stop re-litigating October. The secretary role, upgraded by you. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The event page people keep texting you about** — Your cousin's wedding weekend, the school fest, the campout. Times, addresses, what to wear, all in one link you send instead of retyping. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Carpool rota** — Who drives to practice which day, this month and next, agreed by the parents involved and posted where everyone can see it. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Where the regulars play** — Your pickup game's page: which court or field, which days, when the run actually starts versus when people say it starts. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The QR one-pager** — Your club's table at the fair gets a QR code; this is the page it opens. One screen: what, when, how to join. Built with the club, live before the fair. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Little free library catalog** — What's actually in the little library on your corner this week, checked on your walk home, with a shelf history of what came and went. _(skills: build-website)_
- **House rules, ratified** — The rules your game group argues about every single time, written down, voted on, and published. Disputes now get settled by URL. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The season photo archive** — Every parent has thirty photos on their phone from your team's season. Collect them, pick the good ones, and put the season in one place. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The neighborhood recommendations list** — The plumber, babysitter, and bike shop the adults you know actually vouch for, gathered by asking them, with who recommended what. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Study group hub** — Your actual study group's page: when and where you meet, what got covered, the links and past papers everyone keeps losing in the chat. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The family birthdays page** — Everyone in your extended family: who, when, and what they actually like, built by interviewing your parents. Shared with the relatives who always forget. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The volunteer day recap** — Your group did a real service day. Build the recap page with photos, numbers, and names, and hand it to the organization to share. _(skills: build-website)_

## Selling & services

- **The lawn and yard one-pager** — What you'll do, which streets you cover, and how to text you. Printed as a flyer with the URL, delivered to the houses you can actually walk to. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The babysitting page** — Your real availability, your rates, and short quotes from families you've actually sat for, with their okay. Parents send this to other parents. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The tutoring page** — The one subject you're genuinely good at, your real results as proof, and when you're free. One subject only; depth beats a menu. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The dog page** — Photos of the dogs you already walk or sit, what you charge, and the streets you cover. The dogs' owners are your references; ask them for a line each. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The garage sale catalog** — Your family's actual garage sale, every item photographed and priced, linked from the yard sign. Mark things sold as the morning goes. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The commission page** — You already draw, bake, or make bracelets. Show real finished pieces, say what a request costs, and explain how to ask for one. No promises you haven't tested. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The bike repair page** — You already fix flats and adjust brakes for friends. Write down what you can fix, what you charge, and where to find you on Saturdays. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Tech help for neighbors** — Printers, phones, wifi, the TV remote situation. A page that says what you can fix and what you can't, aimed at the neighbors who already ask. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The camera roll, curated** — You already shoot your friends' games and events. Pick your twenty best shots, put them on a page, and offer to shoot the next one on purpose. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The lessons page** — The instrument you've played for years, offered to beginners: what a lesson covers, when you're free, first one free. A clip of you playing is the proof. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The bilingual help page** — You speak two languages; some of your neighbors' forms, letters, and phone calls only come in one. Say what you'll help with and when. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The moving crew page** — You and two strong friends, available weekends, for the couch-up-the-stairs jobs in your own neighborhood. Names, photo, how to book. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Plant and mail watch** — When neighbors travel, you water, collect mail, and send a photo update. A page with your streets, your rates, and how the photo updates work. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The bake order page** — The thing you already bake well, offered by the batch: what it is, what it costs, how far ahead to ask. Photos of bakes you actually made. _(skills: build-website)_
- **Errands for the block** — Store runs and prescription pickups for the older neighbors who already know you, with a page their kids can read and trust: who you are, what you do, your number via a form your parents okay. _(skills: build-website)_
- **The textbook hand-me-down list** — Everything you and your friends are done with, listed with condition and price, aimed at next year's students at your actual school. _(skills: build-website)_

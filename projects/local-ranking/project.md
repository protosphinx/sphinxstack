## Brief

Every taco, boba, or pizza place within walking distance of school,
visited by you and scored on the same fixed criteria, published as a
table with visit dates. The methodology is the product: same scorer,
same criteria, every visit dated. That is more than most review sites
can say.

## Personalize

- **The category and the boundary.** One food or drink category you
  can afford to sample, inside a boundary you state on the page.
  Every place inside the boundary gets visited; skipping the far one
  breaks the "definitive" claim.
- **Your criteria.** Four or five, decided before the first visit,
  with a defined scale for each. Price paid is a column, since you
  are the one paying.
- **Your tastes, disclosed.** You hate cilantro or love extra-sweet;
  say so on the page so readers can calibrate against you.

## Milestones

1. List every qualifying place; lock the criteria and scales in
   writing before visiting.
2. Do the visits; score each place on-site or the same day, with date
   and price paid.
3. Structure the scores into one data file.
4. Publish the table: sortable or ranked, every score and visit date
   shown, criteria explained above it.
5. Write the short verdict: the winner, the surprise, and anything a
   single visit cannot settle.

## Done means

- Live URL with the full table, criteria definitions, and visit dates
- Every place inside the stated boundary visited and scored; none
  skipped silently
- Criteria locked before visits and unchanged after (or the change is
  disclosed on the page)
- Scores are yours from your visits; nothing imported from review
  sites
- At least 5 commits across at least 4 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

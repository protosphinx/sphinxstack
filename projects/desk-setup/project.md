## Brief

Your desk, documented: every item on and around it, what it is,
what it cost, why it is there, and what you would change. Setup
pages are a whole genre online; yours is the honest version, with
the tangled cables left in the photo and the regret purchases
admitted. It stays alive after launch: when the setup changes, the
page changes.

## Personalize

- **Your desk as it is.** Photograph it before any tidying beyond
  what you would do for a friend visiting. The page documents the
  desk you use.
- **The full inventory.** Everything: the monitor and also the mug,
  the lamp, the thing you fidget with. Real prices, or "gift" or
  "found" where true.
- **The verdicts.** For each item, would you buy it again? The no
  answers are what make the page worth reading.

## Milestones

1. One wide photo and a five-item list live at a URL, day one.
2. Complete inventory with photos of each item at your desk, in
   place.
3. Write-ups: what, cost, why it earned its spot, the would-you-
   rebuy verdict.
4. A wishlist section: what you would change and what stops you.
5. Layout pass: image-heavy pages punish sloppy sizing, so make it
   load fast and read well on a phone.
6. First update shipped after something on the desk changes. Final
   README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; loads fast on a phone
- Every item on the desk listed, photographed in place, priced
- Honest verdicts, including at least one would-not-rebuy
- At least one update after launch reflecting a change on the desk
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

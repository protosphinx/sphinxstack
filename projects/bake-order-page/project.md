## Brief

The thing you already bake well, offered by the batch: what it is,
what a batch costs, and how far ahead to ask. Photos of bakes you
made, and the full ingredient list on the page, because the person
ordering may be feeding someone with an allergy.

## Personalize

- **The bake.** One item you make well, done properly. Cookies you
  have baked thirty times beat a menu of five maybes.
- **The ingredients.** The complete list, with nuts, dairy, eggs, and
  gluten called out. If your kitchen also handles nuts, the page says
  that too.
- **The price.** Cost the ingredients for one batch at the store your
  family uses, add your time, write the math in the README.
- **The rules where you live.** Many places have cottage-food rules
  about selling home baking. Check with a parent what applies, and
  scope the offer (friends and family, or wider) accordingly.

## Milestones

1. Test batch baked, photographed, and costed.
2. Page live by mid-week: photos, ingredient list, price, lead time,
   contact.
3. Local rules checked with a parent; the offer scoped to match.
4. First order taken, baked, and delivered on the promised day.
5. Final README with the batch math and what the first buyer said.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Honest-claims check passed: photos are your own bakes, ingredient
  list is complete
- Allergens called out on the page and repeated when an order is
  taken
- Price covers ingredients, with the math in the README
- First order or inquiry logged in SESSIONS.md, which has an entry
  per session
- Buyers pay you directly on delivery; the page never takes payment

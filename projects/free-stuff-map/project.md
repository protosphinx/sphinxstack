## Brief

A page mapping every water fountain, public bathroom, and free wifi
spot near your school, each one checked on foot by you. The verified
part is the whole product: anyone can copy a list off the internet,
but only you walked there and confirmed the fountain works.

## Personalize

- **Your radius.** Draw the boundary you can cover on foot in a week:
  the blocks around your school, the route home, the park. State the
  boundary on the page.
- **Your categories.** Fountains, bathrooms, and wifi are the starter
  set; swap in what your area has (benches, bike racks, phone
  charging, shade in summer).
- **Your verification note.** For each spot, one line only you could
  write: the fountain with low pressure, the bathroom code, the wifi
  that needs a login.

## Milestones

1. Define the boundary and categories; make the survey sheet you will
   carry (paper or phone note).
2. Walk the area and log every spot: name, location, category, date
   visited, working or not.
3. Structure the log into one data file with coordinates or nearest
   cross-street.
4. Page live: a map or ordered list grouped by category, each spot
   with its visit date and note.
5. Re-verify the three spots most likely to have changed; add a "last
   checked" date to each entry.

## Done means

- Live URL listing every spot inside the stated boundary
- Every entry has a visit date and a note from your own visit
- Nothing on the page is copied from another list unverified
- Broken or closed spots are shown as broken, not deleted
- At least 6 commits across at least 4 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

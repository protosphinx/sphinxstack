## Brief

One dish, cooked start to finish by your grandmother (or whoever
holds the family recipe), filmed by you and cut into a short: her
hands doing the work, her voice over it telling you how and why. You
shoot and edit; the agent helps plan the shot list and reviews cuts.
If you built the family recipe archive, this pairs with it.

## Personalize

- **The dish.** The one only she makes right, ideally the one with a
  story attached. Ask her to pick if you cannot.
- **Her comfort.** Some grandmothers perform for a camera and some
  freeze. Decide together whether she talks while cooking or you
  record her narration afterward.
- **The kitchen.** Hers. Clear one counter angle and one overhead if
  you can manage it. Do not restage her somewhere nicer.

## Milestones

1. The dish and a filming day agreed with her.
2. Shot list: hands, ingredients, the technique moment, the taste at
   the end.
3. The cook filmed, with extra takes of the key steps.
4. Her narration recorded, even two minutes of it.
5. Cut to a short under ninety seconds, her voice over her hands.
6. Her approval on the final cut, then published.

## Done means

- Live at a public URL
- Her hands and voice carry the video; you stay behind the camera
- She saw the final cut and said yes before it went up
- The steps shown are the recipe as she makes it, uncorrected
- Her narration is clear enough to follow

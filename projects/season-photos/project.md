## Brief

Every parent on your team has thirty photos from the season sitting
on their phone, and nobody has all of them. Collect them, pick the
good ones, caption them with the game and date, and put the season
in one place. The team gets the link and finally sees the whole
season instead of their own phone's slice of it.

## Personalize

- **The team and the season.** Your team (or your sibling's), a
  season that is finished or nearly so. The coach or team manager
  knows the plan and okays it before you start collecting.
- **The collection route.** The parents' group chat is usually the
  channel. Ask once, clearly: which games, how to send, by when.
  Expect a trickle and a second ask.
- **The cut.** Thirty photos per parent means hundreds arrive. Pick
  the keepers: sharp, tells a moment, shows someone doing something.
  Credit the photographer under each photo.

## Milestones

1. Coach or manager okays the project; the ask goes to the parents'
   chat.
2. Photos collected and organized by game while they arrive.
3. Consent pass: every player who appears in a kept photo (their
   parent, for younger teammates) has okayed being on the site. Any
   photo without full consent is cut, without exception.
4. Archive live at a URL, organized by game, captioned with dates
   and photographer credits.
5. Coach reviews the whole site before the link goes out.
6. Link shared to the team and parents' chat; corrections and
   additions handled. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; photos organized by game with dates
- Coach or manager sign-off recorded in the README
- Every visible player has consent on record (parent's consent where
  appropriate); every photo credits its photographer
- The link was shared with the team and parents, and at least one
  family sent a reaction or correction, noted in the README
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by
  you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

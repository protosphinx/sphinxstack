## Brief

Every tree on your block: identified with a field guide, photographed,
measured where you can, and published as a map or list backed by a
data file in a public repo. It is real fieldwork at walkable scale,
and the expert check at the end (the neighbor with the garden, a
biology teacher) is part of the method.

## Personalize

- **Your block, exactly.** State the boundary: which side, corner to
  corner. Every tree inside it gets an entry, including the dead one
  and the stump.
- **Your field guide.** A regional guide or a plant-ID app, named on
  the page as your identification source. Where you are unsure,
  record the two candidates instead of forcing a pick.
- **Your checker.** Someone who knows local trees better than you.
  Their corrections get recorded, and they get thanked on the page
  by whatever name they choose.

## Milestones

1. Walk the block once and count; set up the survey sheet: location,
   species guess, trunk girth, condition, photo reference.
2. The survey walk: photograph and log every tree. Shoot leaves and
   bark close-up; that is what identification needs.
3. Identify each tree with the guide; mark confidence per entry
   (sure, probable, unknown).
4. Structure the log into one data file; build the page mapping or
   listing every tree with photo and details.
5. The expert check: walk the block or the page with your checker;
   record every correction.
6. Publish the repo (data file, photos, README on method) and the
   final page.

## Done means

- Live page with every tree in the stated boundary, photos included
- Public repo with the data file and a README describing the method
- Per-tree confidence recorded; unknowns published as unknowns
- Expert check happened and the corrections are in the data
- Photos are yours from the survey walk
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

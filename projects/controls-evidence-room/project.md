## Brief

Choose six to ten real controls for a small application, club,
nonprofit, or lab. Normalize the requirements, identify where each
control is enforced, and assemble a privacy-conscious evidence room
covering one review period. Include at least one automated control,
one manual control, one inherited dependency, and one gap. Reconcile
the relevant population before sampling and test whether the evidence
shows design and operation rather than policy intent alone.

## Personalize

- **The audience.** Pick a real internal reviewer, customer request,
  grant condition, or governance committee.
- **The control set.** Choose objectives that matter to the system's
  actual risks rather than copying a large framework.
- **The independent check.** Ask someone outside the control owner
  group to reproduce one population and one operating test.

## Milestones

1. Scope, period, audience, requirements, and evidence-handling rules approved.
2. Control objectives mapped to risks, activities, owners, systems, and frequencies.
3. Authoritative populations reconciled for identities, changes, assets, or other sampled items.
4. Evidence collected with provenance, period, integrity check, redaction, and access controls.
5. Design and operating tests performed; stale, missing, contradictory, and failed evidence retained.
6. Independent reviewer reproduces one control result and verifies the remediation tracker.

## Done means

- Six to ten control objectives retain source-to-evidence traceability
- Every evidence item has scope, period, collector, source, and sensitivity
- Samples reconcile to an authoritative population
- Missing or failed evidence is visible and never backdated
- Access to the evidence room is tested and later reviewed
- Independent reproduction and remediation verification are recorded

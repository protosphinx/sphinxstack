## Brief

Every decision your club has made, dated and findable at one URL, so
meetings stop re-litigating what was settled in October. One page
per meeting, one line per decision, newest first. This is the
secretary role with a permanent address.

## Personalize

- **The club.** A real club, team, or society you belong to that
  holds meetings and makes decisions. If you are not the secretary,
  the current secretary is your partner and your source.
- **The backlog.** Decide how far back you go. Old paper minutes, a
  notes app, the treasurer's memory: pick a starting point you can
  finish typing in a week.
- **What counts as a decision.** Agree the format with whoever runs
  the club: date, what was decided, who is doing it. Keep debate and
  gossip out; this site records outcomes.

## Milestones

1. Get sign-off from whoever runs the club, and agree what is
   public. Some clubs will want the site private or unlisted;
   respect that choice.
2. One page live at a URL with the most recent meeting's decisions.
3. Backlog entered: past meetings typed in, dated, newest first.
4. Finding things works: a browser text search across one page, or
   a simple search box if you build one. Test it on the decision
   people misremember most.
5. The next meeting happens and its decisions go up within a day.
6. The club is told where the site lives, at a meeting or in the
   club chat. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; every entry dated
- Sign-off from the club's leader on existence and visibility,
  recorded in the README
- At least one meeting was recorded live, published within a day
- Someone other than you has used the site to settle what was
  decided, noted in the README
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by
  you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

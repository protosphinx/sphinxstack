## Brief

The instrument you have played for years, offered to beginners: what
the first lesson covers, when you are free, and a clip of you playing
as the proof. The first lesson is free so nobody has to gamble on an
unknown teacher, and so you find out whether you like teaching before
anyone pays.

## Personalize

- **The instrument and the level.** Absolute beginners up to their
  first full songs. Teach the stretch of the path you remember
  walking; leave advanced students to advanced teachers.
- **The clip.** You, playing, recorded plainly on a phone. One good
  take beats an edited montage.
- **The lesson plan.** What lesson one covers, borrowed from how you
  were taught, minus the parts that bored you.
- **The setting.** Your home with a parent around, the student's home
  with theirs, or a video call. Say which on the page.

## Milestones

1. Page live on day one: times, setting, contact.
2. Clip recorded and embedded.
3. Lesson-one plan written out.
4. Offer shared by mid-week with the school music teacher and family
   friends with kids.
5. First free lesson taught; what landed and what confused the
   student logged.
6. Final README with your rate for lesson two onward and the
   reasoning behind it.

## Done means

- Live URL with a working clip; readable on a phone
- Honest-claims check passed: years played and level claimed match
  reality, and the clip is you
- First lesson or inquiry logged in SESSIONS.md, which has an entry
  per session
- Lessons happen with a parent nearby on both sides, or on a video
  call parents know about
- Payment from lesson two onward, in person, at the rate on the page

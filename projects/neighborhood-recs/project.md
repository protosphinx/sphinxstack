## Brief

The adults around you already know which plumber shows up, which
babysitter to call, and which bike shop is honest. That knowledge
lives in phone contacts and gets shared one favor at a time. Gather
it by asking them, publish it with who vouched for what, and give
your street a directory where every entry is backed by a person.

## Personalize

- **Your sources.** The neighbors, family friends, and relatives you
  can ask directly. Aim for eight to ten households; each interview
  is ten minutes.
- **The categories.** Only what your sources have opinions about.
  If nobody you asked has a roofer, the list has no roofer section.
  An entry without a voucher does not exist.
- **The vouching line.** Each entry carries what the recommender
  said and their name or household. Ask each person how they want to
  be credited; "the Riveras on 5th" and "a neighbor of 12 years" are
  both fine answers.

## Milestones

1. Interview your sources and collect recommendations with the story
   behind each one.
2. Consent pass: every recommender okays their entry and their
   credit line; every listed business or sitter is told they are
   being listed and can decline.
3. Directory live at a URL, grouped by category, each entry with its
   voucher.
4. Contact details checked: numbers dialed or looked up so the page
   does not send anyone to a dead line.
5. Link shared to the neighborhood chat or handed back to the people
   you interviewed.
6. First use: someone hires or calls from the list. Get the story
   and note it. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; every entry has a named or described voucher
- All recommenders consented to their credit lines; listed people
  and businesses were informed, recorded in the README
- Contact details verified within the project week
- The list reached the neighborhood and at least one person used it
  to find a service, noted in the README
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by
  you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

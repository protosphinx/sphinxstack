## Brief

Your pickup game has answers that only exist in the regulars' heads:
which court or field, which days, and when the run starts as opposed
to when people claim it starts. Put those answers on one page so a
newcomer can show up right the first time and a regular can check
whether tonight is on.

## Personalize

- **The game.** A pickup run you attend: basketball, football,
  volleyball, whatever your crowd plays. You need to have been there
  enough times to know the unwritten rules.
- **The regulars' okay.** There is no president, but there are two
  or three people the game revolves around. Tell them the plan and
  get their yes; some games do not want to be findable, and that
  answer ends the project or makes the page unlisted.
- **The honest details.** The stated start versus the real start,
  what happens when it rains, how teams get picked, what to bring.
  This local knowledge is the whole value of the page.

## Milestones

1. Ask the senior regulars for their blessing and collect the
   details you do not already know.
2. One page live at a URL: place, days, the honest start time.
3. The finding-it section done: which entrance, which court number,
   a photo of the spot that you took yourself.
4. The newcomer section: how joining works, skill expectations, the
   rain rule.
5. Reviewed by a senior regular; corrections applied.
6. Link shared where the game coordinates (the chat, or word of
   mouth), and one person uses it to show up. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Blessing from the game's senior regulars recorded in the README
- At least one person found or checked the game through the page,
  noted in the README
- The photo of the spot is yours; no player is named or pictured
  without their okay
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by
  you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

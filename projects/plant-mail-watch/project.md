## Brief

When neighbors travel, you water the plants, collect the mail, and
send a photo update after every visit. The page explains your
streets, your rates, and how the photo updates work, written so a
nervous traveler reading it from an airport relaxes. The photo after
every visit is the product; the watering is the excuse.

## Personalize

- **The visit.** Exactly what one visit includes and how often you
  come. The photo update goes out the same hour, every time.
- **The keys.** Handed over in person, labeled with a code word
  instead of an address, kept at your home, returned by hand when the
  neighbor is back. Write the routine on the page.
- **The rates.** Per visit, set with a parent. A ten-day trip should
  have an obvious total.
- **The proof.** A sample photo update on the page, from a practice
  run at your own house or a relative's.

## Milestones

1. Page live on day one: streets, rates, how updates work.
2. Practice run done at your own or a relative's house; the sample
   update made from it.
3. Key routine written and checked with a parent.
4. Page shared by mid-week with the neighbors who already know your
   family.
5. First trip covered start to finish: every visit done, every visit
   photographed.
6. Final README: trips covered, what a traveler asked before saying
   yes.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone
- Honest-claims check passed: the sample update is from a run you
  did, the streets listed are ones you can reach daily
- Keys handled per the written routine: no address on the label,
  returned by hand
- First job or inquiry logged in SESSIONS.md, which has an entry per
  session, with a photo per visit
- Payment agreed before the trip and settled in person after
- Mail collected and held unopened

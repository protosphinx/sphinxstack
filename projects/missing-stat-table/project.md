## Brief

Your school or rec league does not publish the table you want:
assists, saves, head-to-head records. Build it yourself from game
records and keep it current through the season. Because you are the
only source, the bar for accuracy is higher, so every number needs a
stated origin.

## Personalize

- **The stat with a gap.** It must be genuinely unpublished (check
  first) and compilable from records you can reach: scorebooks,
  league pages, your own game notes.
- **Your sources, ranked.** Official scorebook beats your notes beats
  a teammate's memory. Write the ranking on the page and tag each
  entry with its source grade.
- **The people in the table.** Stats about schoolmates are about
  people; use the names the league already publishes, and honor
  removal requests.

## Milestones

1. Confirm the table does not exist; inventory which game records you
   can access.
2. Compile the season so far into one data file, each figure tagged
   with its source.
3. Publish the table, sorted by the stat, with the source ranking and
   a last-updated date on the page.
4. Update after the next two rounds of games, within two days of each.
5. Show it to a coach, scorekeeper, or the league's most obsessive
   fan; correct what they catch.

## Done means

- Live URL with the full table and a visible last-updated date
- Every figure traceable to a tagged source; estimates marked as
  estimates
- Updated at least twice after launch, proving the keeping-current
  part
- One knowledgeable person has checked it and corrections were made
- At least 6 commits across at least 4 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

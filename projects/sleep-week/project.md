## Brief

A notebook by the bed, seven nights of numbers, then a chart and one
honest paragraph about what the data says versus what you tell people.
Most people claim a number ("I get about seven hours"); this project
checks yours against a week of evidence.

## Personalize

- **Your claim, on record.** Before night one, write down what you
  believe your average is. That sentence goes on the page next to
  the measured result.
- **Your measures.** Lights-out time, wake time, and a 1-to-5 rested
  score are enough. A phone or watch tracker can run alongside as a
  second opinion, but the notebook is the primary record.
- **Your disclosure line.** Sleep data is health-adjacent. Publish
  the numbers, or publish the write-up with the table summarized;
  either is a valid project.

## Milestones

1. Write the pre-registration: your claimed average, the measures,
   and the seven nights, dated before you start.
2. Collect: notebook entry every morning within an hour of waking.
3. Structure the entries into one data file; compute the average and
   the spread.
4. Chart the week, zero-based axis, every night shown.
5. Publish: chart, table (or summary), and the paragraph comparing
   claim to measurement.

## Done means

- Live URL with the pre-registered claim and the measured result side
  by side
- Seven consecutive mornings logged; any gap shown as a gap
- The paragraph reports the comparison without excuses or promises
- Notebook (or a photo of it) kept as the primary source
- At least 4 commits across at least 3 distinct days, authored by you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

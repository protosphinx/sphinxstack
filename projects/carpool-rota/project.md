## Brief

Who drives to practice which day, this month and next, posted where
every family can see it. The rota itself is agreed by the parents;
your job is to make the agreement visible, current, and impossible
to misremember. When a driver swap happens, the page changes the
same day.

## Personalize

- **The carpool.** A real recurring run: practice, rehearsal,
  weekend games. You know the families because you are in one of the
  cars.
- **The parent in charge.** One parent already coordinates this,
  probably by group text. They are your source for the schedule and
  they approve the page before other families get the link.
- **What a row needs.** Date, driver, pickup time. Ask the parents
  what else they always have to ask about, and include only that.

## Milestones

1. Get the current arrangement from the coordinating parent and
   their yes to putting it at a URL. Agree what stays off the page;
   phone numbers and addresses likely stay in the group text.
2. This month's rota live at a URL, checked by the coordinator.
3. Next month added once the parents settle it.
4. A visible "last updated" date, so families can trust the page is
   current.
5. Link shared to the parents' group chat by the coordinator.
6. First swap handled: a driver changes, the page updates the same
   day, and the chat gets a one-line heads-up. Final README.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; readable on a phone in a parking lot
- Coordinator's sign-off recorded in the README
- Parents received the link and at least one has checked the page
  instead of asking the chat, noted in the README
- A schedule change was reflected on the page the day it happened
- No phone numbers or home addresses on the page
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by
  you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

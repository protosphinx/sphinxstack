## Brief

Your group did a service day: a park cleanup, a food drive, a
painting day at the shelter. Build the recap page with photos, the
numbers (bags collected, meals packed, hours worked), and the names
of who showed up. Then hand it to the organization so they can share
it. The page is a gift to them, which means they get final say over
it.

## Personalize

- **The day and the organization.** A service day that already
  happened or is about to, with an organization on the other end.
  The organization's contact person is your client; introduce
  yourself and the idea before building.
- **The numbers.** Ask the organizer what they count. Organizations
  report to funders in numbers, and the recap that includes theirs
  is the recap they will use.
- **The photo and name rules.** Volunteers are okayed individually;
  photos taken at a shelter or with an organization's clients may be
  off-limits entirely. The organization's rules win, and asking
  first is part of the project.

## Milestones

1. Contact the organization, pitch the recap, and learn their photo
   and naming rules.
2. Gather the material: photos from the day, the counts, the
   volunteer list.
3. Consent pass: every named or pictured volunteer says yes;
   anything failing the organization's rules is cut.
4. Recap live at a URL: what happened, the numbers, the photos, a
   thank-you list.
5. The organization reviews it; their edits applied; their sign-off
   recorded.
6. Handoff: the organization shares the link through their own
   channel (newsletter, social, site), and your group gets it too.
   Final README.
7. Add a private achievement-inventory entry linking the live page,
   organization sign-off, consent record, share evidence, and your
   exact contribution.

## Done means

- Live URL responding; numbers and date accurate per the organizer
- Organization's sign-off recorded in the README
- Every named or pictured person consented; the organization's photo
  rules were followed, noted in the README
- The organization shared the link through a channel of theirs, with
  evidence in the README
- A private achievement-inventory entry links the result and evidence
  without copying volunteer data
- At least 8 commits across at least 5 distinct days, authored by
  you
- SESSIONS.md has an entry per session, written by you

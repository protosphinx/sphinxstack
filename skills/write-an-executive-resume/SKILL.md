# write-an-executive-resume

## Procedure

1. Define target mandate, audience, sector, stage, ownership context, geography, and document length.
2. Build an evidence ledger for roles, reporting lines, budgets, teams, regions, products, risk, transformations, and outcomes.
3. Separate direct accountability, shared leadership, sponsorship, governance, advisory input, and team execution.
4. Lead with the current through-line and the few capabilities most relevant to the mandate.
5. Write role entries around context, decisions, operating mechanisms, outcomes, and lessons rather than duty lists.
6. Normalize currencies, periods, definitions, acquisitions, inherited baselines, and estimates.
7. Remove confidential board, employee, customer, deal, security, and nonpublic financial detail.
8. Verify every number, title, date, organization, claim, and public link with the person and sources.

## Guardrails

- Do not inflate team size, budget, ownership, turnaround, revenue, valuation, savings, or board authority.
- Never expose nonpublic strategy, transactions, people, customers, or regulated information.
- Do not attribute market movement or team outcomes solely to one executive without evidence.

## Done

- An executive resume document traces scope, decisions, outcomes, and authority to verified evidence
- Direct, shared, sponsored, advisory, and team contributions are distinguished
- Every number, date, title, claim, privacy boundary, and link is checked

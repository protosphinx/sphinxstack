# navigate-a-background-check
## When to use
- Use after receiving the scope, provider, consent request, and role context.
- Obtain qualified legal support for rights or adverse action questions.
## Procedure
1. Verify requester, provider, scope, purpose, jurisdiction, consent, data, and deadlines.
2. Prepare truthful identity variants, employment, education, licenses, and requested records.
3. Share only necessary information through the authorized secure channel.
4. Record submissions and request copies or status where rights allow.
5. Review matches for identity, dates, source, completeness, and context.
6. Respond to discrepancies with evidence and preserve the process.
## Failure plan
- Stop and seek support for suspicious providers, overbroad data, identity theft, or denied process rights.
## Worked example
A namesake criminal record is disputed with identity evidence before an adverse decision.
## Done
- A background-check record lists requester, scope, consent, data, documents, submissions, discrepancies, responses, and outcome
- Provider, identity, privacy, secure-channel, source, deadline, fair-response, and retention checks verify process

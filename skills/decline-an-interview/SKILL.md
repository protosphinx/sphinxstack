# decline-an-interview

Close the loop without manufacturing an explanation.

## When to use

- Use before or after scheduling when withdrawing is the current decision.
- Use a rescheduling request instead when interest remains and only the timing changed.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the exact role, company, contact, interview time, and whether any travel, assignment, or account is outstanding.
2. Respond promptly in the verified thread.
3. State the withdrawal or decline in the first two sentences.
4. Thank the contact for the specific time or consideration already given.
5. Give a short truthful reason only if useful; no private detail is required.
6. Cancel the calendar invitation after sending and close any agreed logistical action.
7. Ask how submitted samples or personal data are handled if that matters to the decision.
8. Update the application tracker with date, decision, and any future-contact preference.

## Done

- A sent message unambiguously declines the interview, identifies the role, and closes relevant logistics
- The calendar, tracker, assignments, expenses, data questions, and future-contact preference have a recorded disposition

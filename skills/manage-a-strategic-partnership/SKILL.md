# manage-a-strategic-partnership

Maintain shared value without blurring each party's authority and obligations.

## When to use

- Use for distribution, platform, research, product, market, public-private, or ecosystem partnerships.
- Do not share customer, employee, confidential, or competitively sensitive data beyond approved purpose.

## Procedure

1. Confirm strategy, mutual outcomes, parties, entities, scope, exclusions, conflicts, and authorized agreement.
2. Map contributions, dependencies, intellectual property, data, brand, customer ownership, pricing, funding, risk, and benefit.
3. Define sponsors, operating leads, decision rights, reserved matters, quorum, escalation, and dispute handling.
4. Establish workstreams, interfaces, milestones, acceptance, service levels, reporting, and communication.
5. Implement security, privacy, consent, competition, accessibility, safety, compliance, records, and subcontractor controls.
6. Track leading and outcome measures, financial reconciliation, customer effects, incidents, obligations, and value distribution.
7. Govern changes, new use cases, conflicts, exclusivity, public claims, and material underperformance.
8. Rehearse pause and exit, including data, access, customers, staff, assets, intellectual property, communication, and continuing duties.

## Done

- A partnership operating plan records outcomes, contributions, governance, interfaces, controls, measures, changes, conflicts, and exit
- Agreement, decision-right, data, financial, service, customer, public-claim, access, and exit-readiness checks verify operation

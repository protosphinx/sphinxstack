# manage-a-job-search-confidentially
## When to use
- Use when discovery could cause retaliation, conflict, visa, safety, or relationship risk.
- Do not take proprietary data, misuse employer systems, or ask others to lie.
## Procedure
1. Assess exposure channels, consequences, obligations, references, and safe support.
2. Use personal devices, accounts, storage, network, calendar, and contact details.
3. Control resume visibility, platform signals, recruiter disclosure, and reference timing.
4. Schedule interviews lawfully and neutrally.
5. Keep work samples free of confidential or customer material.
6. Plan notice, transition, counteroffer, and accidental disclosure response.
## Failure plan
- If safety or legal risk is high, obtain qualified confidential support before acting.
## Worked example
A candidate disables public profile signals and asks recruiters not to contact the employer before consent.
## Done
- A confidential-search plan records risks, channels, disclosure rules, references, logistics, data boundaries, and response
- Device, account, network, profile, recruiter, reference, sample, calendar, and accidental-disclosure checks verify protection

# color-correct-video-footage

Normalize through a managed color pipeline and judge with scopes plus calibrated viewing.

## Procedure

1. Confirm camera, codec, transfer function, gamut, metadata, timeline, monitor, and delivery space.
2. Apply the correct input transform without baking the same conversion twice.
3. Set black, white, exposure, and contrast while preserving recoverable detail.
4. Correct white balance and color casts against known neutrals and scene intent.
5. Check skin, products, brand colors, and memory colors with scopes and references.
6. Match adjacent shots for exposure, balance, saturation, and continuity.
7. Separate technical correction from the creative grade and keep changes reversible.
8. Test highlights, shadows, noise, mixed light, graphics, and output transforms.
9. Export stills and a correction report with settings and known limitations.

## Guardrails

- Do not assume camera metadata or automatic transforms are correct.
- Avoid making skin or environment conform to biased aesthetic defaults.
- Never judge critical color on an unverified display alone.

## Done

- A corrected timeline and color-pipeline report are saved
- Input transforms, scopes, skin, references, and shot continuity are verified
- Output renders are checked in the actual delivery color space

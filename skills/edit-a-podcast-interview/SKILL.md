# edit-a-podcast-interview

Shape the listening experience while keeping every edit traceable to the source.

## Procedure

1. Preserve originals and create synchronized working copies, transcript, and source log.
2. Read the full interview and mark story, evidence, context, repetition, sensitivity, and follow-up.
3. Build a radio edit that preserves chronology or explains deliberate restructuring.
4. Remove repetition and verbal clutter without manufacturing fluency or certainty.
5. Use room tone, breaths, pauses, music, and narration transparently.
6. Fact-check claims, names, dates, clips, and required context.
7. Apply participant, editorial, legal, rights, and safety decisions.
8. Clean dialogue, balance levels, and listen through every cut with headphones and speakers.
9. Export review and final versions with an edit decision record.

## Guardrails

- Never splice words to create a statement the speaker did not make.
- Do not remove uncertainty or qualification that changes meaning.
- Keep source audio and sensitive review files access-controlled.

## Done

- A source-traceable edit decision list and episode master are saved
- Meaning, facts, rights, consent, and technical continuity are verified
- The encoded final is listened to beginning to end

# resume-review

Review a resume that already exists, the way a screener
and then a skeptical interviewer would. The kindness is in the
bluntness: they are about to send this to strangers who will not
explain why they said no. Done means every problem you can fix
together is fixed, and the ones you cannot are named.

## Ground rules

- Verdicts with reasons, line by line. "This bullet says nothing a
  stranger can check" beats "looks pretty good overall". Never
  soften a real problem into a suggestion.
- Fixes use only material that is true. Rewording weak phrasing is
  in scope; inventing a metric to strengthen a bullet is not. When
  a line needs evidence that does not exist, the verdict is "cut
  or go build the evidence", not "add a number".
- Fix as you go. Each verdict comes with the repair, made now with
  their yes — this is a working session, not a report.

## The six-second pass

First, read it the way a screener does — six seconds, then
verdict. Report exactly what registered: the name, the first
section header, maybe two bullets, whether it looked crowded.
Then the layout facts that decide the six seconds: over one page,
dense walls of text, tiny fonts, columns or graphics that break
ATS parsing, an unprofessional email address, a photo. Each is a
fix made now.

## The line pass

Go bullet by bullet. For each line, one verdict:

- Keep — specific, true, and carries a link or checkable fact.
- Rewrite — a real thing said weakly. "Responsible for social
  media" hides "ran the club account, three posts a week"; ask
  what actually happened and reword to the fact. Aspiration verbs
  ("passionate", "motivated") become evidence-backed action verbs or are removed.
- Needs evidence — a claim with nothing behind it. A skills line
  no other line supports, a project with no link, an achievement
  with no artifact. They either supply the evidence now or the
  line moves to Cut.
- Cut — objectives paragraphs, word-cloud skills, "references
  available", middle-school entries, anything they could not
  discuss for two minutes in an interview.

Also check the spine: dates consistent and gap-free or gaps
ownable, tenses consistent, zero typos (read it aloud), contact
links all working.

## Done

- Every line carries a verdict, and every Keep/Rewrite/Cut is
  applied to the file
- One page, ATS-safe, links tested, exported to fresh PDF
- A short honest list of Needs-evidence gaps that survive — with
  the fix for each being something to build, not something to
  phrase (https://sphinxstack.com/skills/)
- If the review removed most of the page, hand off to build-resume
  and rebuild from the interview up

If they are targeting one specific posting, run tailor-resume next
on the newly cleaned file.

# explain-an-employment-gap

Explain only what is useful and safe to disclose.

## When to use

- Use for caregiving, health, study, relocation, redundancy, job search, independent work, travel, or other career breaks.
- Do not invent consulting, hide dates deceptively, disclose diagnoses unnecessarily, or shame the candidate for a lawful break.

## Procedure

1. Verify dates and determine whether the gap needs explanation in the resume, cover letter, application, or interview.
2. Identify the minimum truthful context that resolves likely confusion.
3. Inventory relevant study, care, community, project, contract, recovery, or skill-maintenance activity without manufacturing employment.
4. Write a neutral label and one or two factual lines when an entry helps.
5. Protect medical, family, legal, financial, and third-party privacy.
6. Connect current readiness to evidence without claiming the gap caused unrelated strengths.
7. Save the approved wording in an application document and rehearse a concise spoken explanation plus a boundary for intrusive questions.
8. Check dates across resume, profiles, forms, and references.

## Done

- A gap explanation states accurate dates, proportionate context, and relevant current readiness
- Resume, application, profile, and reference timelines are checked for consistency and unnecessary private detail

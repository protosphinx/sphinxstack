# produce-a-livestream

Design the audience experience and failure behavior before connecting the encoder.

## Procedure

1. Define program, audience, platforms, rights, access, latency, recording, and success measures.
2. Map cameras, audio, playback, graphics, captions, switcher, encoder, network, platform, and monitoring.
3. Assign producer, director, technical, audio, graphics, caption, moderation, talent, and incident roles.
4. Prepare run of show, cues, assets, backup content, holding states, and communication channels.
5. Build redundant power, network, encoding, playback, recording, and credential paths proportionate to risk.
6. Configure privacy, moderation, delay, chat, access, and participant safety.
7. Rehearse the full show plus loss of each critical source and operator.
8. Monitor program and return feeds independently from representative audience locations.
9. Record locally, verify the archive, reconcile incidents, and publish an operations report.

## Guardrails

- Never rely solely on the same platform preview that receives the stream.
- Do not display private backstage communications or unapproved participant data.
- Keep safety and editorial stop authority clear during live pressure.

## Done

- A livestream production plan and signal map are approved
- Program, accessibility, moderation, backup, and failure paths are rehearsed
- Independent audience playback, local recording, and incident logs are verified

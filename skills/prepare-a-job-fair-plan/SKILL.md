# prepare-a-job-fair-plan
## When to use
- Use for campus, industry, virtual, or community job fairs.
- Do not collect private recruiter information or monopolize access.
## Procedure
1. Verify event format, employers, roles, schedule, access, and application rules.
2. Rank a small target list plus alternatives.
3. Prepare one true introduction and two role-specific evidence points.
4. Write questions not answered by public material.
5. Prepare resume or links in requested formats.
6. Plan arrival, breaks, accessibility, notes, and follow-up.
## Failure plan
- If a target cancels, use the alternative list rather than spraying every booth.
## Worked example
A candidate prioritizes five employers, prepares questions about team work, and records consented follow-up details.
## Done
- A job-fair plan lists targets, evidence, questions, materials, logistics, access, notes, and follow-up
- Employer, role, schedule, format, accessibility, link, privacy, and follow-up checks verify readiness

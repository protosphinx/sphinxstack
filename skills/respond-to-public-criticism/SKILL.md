# respond-to-public-criticism

Address affected people and facts before optimizing optics. A public reply is one option, not the default.

## When to use

- Use when criticism could materially affect people, trust, operations, safety, rights, or public understanding.
- Use ordinary support handling for a private issue that does not need public coordination.

## Preconditions

- Confirm response authority, investigation owner, affected-person support, evidence access, privacy limits, and required specialist review.
- Preserve the public material before edits or deletions change the available record.

## Procedure

1. Preserve the original content, context, reach, timestamps, edits, linked evidence, and immediate safety or privacy risk.
2. Build a claim ledger separating observation, opinion, allegation, verified fact, contradiction, missing evidence, and harmful disclosure.
3. Identify affected people, duties, ongoing harm, support needs, and who has authority to investigate and speak.
4. Verify relevant records and invite internal disagreement without exposing private sources.
5. Make a proportionate response decision: acknowledge, correct, apologize, explain, act, contact privately, refer, monitor, or do not amplify.
6. Prioritize corrective action and direct support before a polished statement.
7. Draft for the affected audience: what is known, what was wrong, what is being done, what remains unknown, and when evidence will update.
8. Protect legal rights, privacy, safety, security, due process, and whistleblowing without using them as vague shields.
9. Obtain accountable approval, publish through the authoritative channel, preserve versions, and monitor material questions.
10. Complete action verification, issue explicit corrections, and review whether the response reduced harm or merely attention.

## Failure plan

- Do not threaten, expose, mobilize followers against, or speculate about a critic.
- Stop publication when a factual claim is unverified, a person could be endangered, or due process would be compromised.
- Avoid deleting criticism under organizational control unless a clear rule and proportionate moderation process applies.
- If no safe public detail is available, acknowledge the boundary and provide a verified process and update condition.

## Done

- A claim evidence record preserves each claim, source, evidence status, affected people, and gap
- A response decision explains why speaking or not speaking is proportionate
- Published statements are versioned, approved, and fact-checked
- Correction and follow-through proof verifies promised actions and material updates

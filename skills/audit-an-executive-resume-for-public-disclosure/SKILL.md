# audit-an-executive-resume-for-public-disclosure

Public urgency does not reduce the evidence required for material credentials.

## When to use

- Use before appointment announcements, regulatory filings, investor disclosures, board elections, public tenders, or sensitive executive searches.
- Obtain qualified legal, regulatory, background, privacy, board-governance, and communications review.

## Preconditions

- Establish independent governance, verification, legal, privacy, communications, records, and executive-contact authority.
- Define public claims, jurisdictions, filing or announcement deadline, materiality, privacy limits, consent, conflicts, and stop authority.
- Preserve every submitted resume, biography, questionnaire, prior public version, edit, source, and approval.

## Procedure

Complete **claim and identity inventory**, **independent verification**, **conflict, privacy, and disclosure review**, and **approved release and correction** before publication.

1. Build an **executive claim verification register** for identity, names, dates, employers, titles, responsibilities, education, credentials, boards, investments, awards, publications, military or public service, sanctions, litigation, investigations, and conflicts.
2. Compare the resume, biography, board questionnaire, filings, employment records, background materials, public profiles, interviews, and prior disclosures without treating repetition as corroboration.
3. Verify identity and name variants before matching records; protect sensitive identifiers and use authorized sources.
4. Before drawing a negative inference from absent records, document sources searched, names and identities used, jurisdiction and date coverage, retention limits, query method, custodian authority, alternative repositories, and record completeness.
5. Treat `not found` as inconclusive unless an authoritative custodian confirms complete coverage for the identified person, claim, and period; give the executive the source description and proposed classification through the fair-response process, then require independent approval for any adverse conclusion.
6. Verify employment through reliable employer or records evidence, distinguishing legal employer, brand, consulting, advisory, interim, secondment, board, and unpaid roles.
7. Verify education, degree, attendance, honors, licenses, certifications, issuers, jurisdictions, status, dates, and restrictions through authoritative records where available.
8. Verify board, ownership, investment, transaction, related-party, competitor, political, charitable, vendor, family, and other material conflicts under applicable rules.
9. Review material legal, regulatory, disciplinary, insolvency, sanction, debarment, and public-record claims with qualified authority and identity matching.
10. Document confidential work through truthful bounded descriptions and independent evidence without exposing protected information.
11. Classify discrepancies as format, omission, ambiguity, unverifiable, inaccurate, misleading, identity mismatch, or material conflict; give the executive a fair documented response route.
12. Correct verified errors, remove unsupported claims, qualify ambiguity, and escalate material discrepancies independently from appointment advocates.
13. Reconcile the approved resume, biography, filing, announcement, website, investor, media, and board versions to one claim set.
14. Bind approvals to exact versions and prepare correction, notification, and withdrawal action if new evidence emerges.

## Failure plan

- If a material claim cannot be verified before deadline, omit or qualify it rather than imply confirmation.
- If identity matching is uncertain, do not attribute an adverse or favorable record until independently resolved.
- If appointment sponsors are conflicted, transfer verification and disclosure decisions to independent governance.
- If a published claim proves wrong, preserve the original, correct every derivative visibly, and assess filing or stakeholder duties.

## Worked example

A listed company is appointing a senior executive when the submitted resume, biographies, prior filings, media profiles, and board questionnaire conflict about degree completion, employer dates, titles, board service, advisory work, licenses, awards, related-party interests, and confidential projects, while market disclosure and press deadlines are near. Independent reviewers establish identity, verify each material claim from authoritative evidence, classify discrepancies, manage sponsor conflicts, approve one claim set, and prepare derivative correction.

## Done

- An executive claim verification register records every public claim, source, search coverage, record completeness, custodian confirmation, absence status, negative-inference approval, discrepancy, owner, response, privacy treatment, and approval
- A source and discrepancy report proves identity, employment, education, credentials, boards, conflicts, legal records, confidential work, dates, and unresolved limits
- An approved disclosure and correction package demonstrates exact-version consistency across resume, biography, filing, board, investor, media, and web surfaces with independent approval and correction readiness

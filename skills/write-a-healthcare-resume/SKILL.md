# write-a-healthcare-resume

Describe clinical scope without exposing patients or overstating authorization.

## When to use

- Use for nursing, allied health, medicine, pharmacy, clinical research, health operations, or care roles.
- Do not include patient identifiers, confidential cases, expired credentials as active, or procedures outside authorized scope.

## Procedure

1. Confirm role, jurisdiction, setting, credential requirements, competencies, shifts, and application instructions.
2. Verify licenses, registrations, certifications, training, renewal dates, restrictions, and safe verification routes.
3. Inventory clinical settings, populations, acuity, caseload, procedures, equipment, documentation, and multidisciplinary work.
4. Select examples that prove safety, quality, communication, escalation, and relevant clinical judgment.
5. Quantify only aggregate, permissioned workload or outcomes and avoid re-identifiable rare cases.
6. Distinguish observed, assisted, supervised, and independently authorized procedures.
7. Include quality improvement, teaching, leadership, research, or systems work with fair attribution.
8. Match role language naturally and confirm employment, education, credential, and scope dates.
9. Save the resume file and conduct patient-privacy and professional-regulation review before release.

## Done

- A healthcare resume presents verified credentials, authorized scope, settings, populations, safety, quality, and teamwork
- Patient privacy, procedure level, outcomes, dates, restrictions, and official credential status are checked

# moderate-an-online-community

Protect people and participation while separating observed behavior from assumptions about identity or intent.

## Procedure

1. Preserve the report, relevant content, context, timestamps, prior actions, rule version, and reporter safety needs.
2. Assess urgent threats to life, physical safety, privacy, account security, or financial harm through the authorized emergency path.
3. Determine scope, audience, reach, target, repetition, coordination, and potential continuing harm.
4. Match observed content or behavior to the exact current rule and evidence threshold.
5. Check context, translation, satire, counterspeech, quotation, newsworthiness, and account compromise where relevant.
6. Choose no action, label, limit, remove, warn, temporary restrict, permanent ban, or specialist escalation proportionately.
7. Apply the action consistently and preserve the enforcement event.
8. Notify affected parties with the rule, action, duration, appeal path, and safety resources appropriate to the case.
9. Review conflicts, bias, repeat patterns, and difficult cases with a second authorized moderator.
10. Track appeals, reversals, recurrence, rule ambiguity, and community-level prevention.

## Guardrails

- Do not expose reporters, private information, or unnecessary evidence to the accused party or public.
- Never infer protected identity or mental state beyond provided evidence.
- Use approved emergency and hotline procedures for imminent safety risk.
- Avoid punitive engagement or public argument from moderator accounts.

## Done

- A moderation record links evidence, rule, context, action, authority, and notification
- Urgent safety and privacy checks are completed
- Proportionality and consistency receive a second review when required
- Appeal and later outcome are recorded

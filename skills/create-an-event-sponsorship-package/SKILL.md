# create-an-event-sponsorship-package

## Procedure

1. Define event outcomes, audience, sponsor fit, values, prohibited relationships, and approval authority.
2. Inventory scarce and scalable benefits across recognition, content, space, hospitality, access, and follow-up.
3. Price packages from delivery cost, scarcity, audience value, risk, and alternatives rather than invented reach.
4. Specify deliverables, deadlines, asset rules, measurement, dependencies, substitutions, and cancellation.
5. Separate sponsorship from editorial control, speaker selection, attendee data, endorsements, and procurement decisions.
6. Include accessibility, privacy, safety, sustainability, conflicts, and reputation review.
7. State audience and performance estimates with definitions, ranges, and evidence limits.
8. Route each agreement into delivery, invoicing, fulfilment, reporting, and closeout tracking.

## Guardrails

- Do not sell attendee data, implied endorsement, editorial control, or guaranteed outcomes without valid authority and basis.
- Never overstate attendance, impressions, participant identity, exclusivity, or measurement.
- Use approved contract, tax, branding, privacy, and sector-specific review.

## Done

- A sponsorship package lists priced deliverables, evidence, exclusions, and participant protections
- Claims, inventory, fulfilment capacity, approvals, and conflicts are checked
- Signed commitments reconcile to the sponsor delivery tracker and invoice plan

# write-a-sales-resume

Make every performance number comparable and attributable.

## When to use

- Use for quota-carrying, account-management, partnership, presales, or sales leadership roles.
- Do not expose customer terms, confidential pipeline, personal data, or team revenue as individual production.

## Procedure

1. Define target segment, motion, product, territory, deal size, cycle, channel, and leadership expectations.
2. Inventory quota basis, attainment, bookings, recognized revenue, retention, expansion, pipeline, win rate, and period.
3. Reconcile performance to dashboards, compensation statements, awards, or approved manager records.
4. Label currency, fiscal period, new versus renewal, individual versus team, and sourced versus influenced amounts.
5. Describe territory difficulty, sales cycle, stakeholders, product complexity, and candidate actions.
6. Show prospecting, discovery, negotiation, forecasting, handoff, and account outcomes relevant to the role.
7. Generalize customer identities and deal details within confidentiality rules.
8. Remove vanity pipeline, cherry-picked months, double-counted revenue, and unsupported causality.
9. Check every number and award against the private evidence file.

## Done

- A sales resume states comparable quota, attainment, revenue, territory, cycle, and contribution evidence
- Metrics, periods, currencies, customer privacy, team attribution, and source records are reconciled

# write-a-resume-for-public-sector-role
## When to use
- Use with the official posting, criteria, instructions, and candidate history.
- Do not invent clearance, authority, public impact, metrics, or eligibility.
## Procedure
1. Extract essential criteria, behaviors, duties, format, limits, and deadline.
2. Map each criterion to verified context, action, result, and evidence.
3. Preserve organization, role, date, scope, contribution, and outcome.
4. Emphasize accountability, service users, inclusion, stewardship, and lawful delivery where supported.
5. Follow required chronology, anonymity, page, declaration, and accessibility rules.
6. Check every claim and candidate approval.
## Failure plan
- Leave an unsupported criterion honest rather than manufacturing evidence.
## Worked example
A nonprofit coordinator maps procurement and accessibility work to stated competencies without claiming civil-service authority.
## Done
- A public-sector resume document records criteria mapping, verified roles, outcomes, accountability, chronology, and required declarations
- Eligibility, criterion, source, authority, metric, format, anonymity, accessibility, and candidate-approval checks verify compliance

# build-a-professional-sponsorship-plan
## When to use
- Use for career growth where decision-makers need evidence and advocates.
- Do not manufacture intimacy, transactional favors, or bypass fair processes.
## Procedure
1. Define goals, decisions, evidence gaps, and organizational context.
2. Map people who directly know the work and can responsibly advocate.
3. Strengthen visible contribution and documented outcomes first.
4. Request feedback, stretch opportunities, introductions, or advocacy specifically.
5. Make contribution and reciprocity genuine, not quid pro quo.
6. Track consent, commitments, conflicts, outcomes, and dependence risk.
## Failure plan
- Diversify support if one sponsor controls opportunity or creates unsafe obligation.
## Worked example
A manager who observed cross-team delivery advocates for a candidate after the candidate asks directly and shares accurate evidence.
## Done
- A sponsorship plan records goals, evidence, relationships, asks, consent, reciprocity, conflicts, actions, and reviews
- Observation, truth, fairness, power, consent, conflict, dependency, and outcome checks verify ethical support

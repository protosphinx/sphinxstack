# create-a-release-form

Document informed permission without pretending one template fits every jurisdiction or vulnerable participant.

## Procedure

1. Identify production, publisher, participant, contributor role, and qualified legal owner.
2. Describe recording types, editorial purpose, channels, territories, duration, and audiences.
3. State whether name, likeness, voice, contribution, location, or submitted material is covered.
4. Record compensation, expenses, credit, confidentiality, and agreed limitations.
5. Explain editing, context, reuse, withdrawal requests, and what cannot be withdrawn after publication.
6. Add accessibility, translation, age, guardian, and capacity handling where applicable.
7. Provide contact, signature, date, version, and participant copy.
8. Obtain qualified review and reconcile signed forms with production records.

## Guardrails

- This workflow does not replace jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
- Never ask a person to sign language they cannot understand or access.
- A release does not excuse deceptive, defamatory, unsafe, or harmful use.

## Done

- A reviewed release form records parties, uses, limits, compensation, and contacts
- Participant identity, capacity, accessibility, version, and signature are verified
- Signed release records reconcile with captured and published material

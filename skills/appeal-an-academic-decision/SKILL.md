# appeal-an-academic-decision

Build the appeal around the permitted grounds and record. This procedure organizes a case; it does not promise reversal or replace qualified advice.

## Procedure

1. Obtain the decision notice, current appeal policy, grounds, deadline, format, evidence rules, authority, and available support.
2. Preserve the original record, communications, submissions, feedback, timestamps, and relevant accommodation or procedural documents.
3. Separate undisputed facts, disputed facts, interpretations, and new evidence.
4. Map each proposed ground to the exact policy language and supporting record.
5. Build a dated chronology using neutral descriptions and precise document locators.
6. State the requested remedy and show why it follows from the permitted ground.
7. Draft a concise appeal: decision, ground, facts, evidence, policy connection, remedy, and attachments.
8. Remove speculation about motives and unsupported comparisons with other students.
9. Have an authorized adviser or support service check process, privacy, and completeness where available.
10. Submit through the official channel, save proof, and track acknowledgment and response dates.

## Failure plan

- Do not alter evidence, coordinate false accounts, retaliate, or disclose unrelated student information.
- Never present disagreement with judgment as a procedural error unless the policy makes it appealable.
- If safety, discrimination, disability, immigration, or legal rights are involved, seek appropriate current professional guidance.
- If the deadline may expire, submit a truthful protective notice and request instructions.

## Done

- Timeliness, permitted grounds, evidence, and requested remedy are checked against current policy
- Every factual statement links to the preserved record or is labeled as the appellant's account
- Submission and all follow-up evidence are saved

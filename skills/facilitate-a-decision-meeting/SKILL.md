# facilitate-a-decision-meeting

Facilitate the decision rule, not a contest of airtime. Confirm that the room has the authority and evidence to decide.

## Procedure

1. Obtain the decision statement, owner, deadline, scope, criteria, options, evidence, and pre-read.
2. Confirm who decides, who advises, who executes, and how dissent or conflicts are handled.
3. Open by restating the decision, fixed constraints, unknowns, and definition of a valid outcome.
4. Check pre-read comprehension and correct factual disagreements before advocacy.
5. Compare each viable option against the same criteria, risks, dependencies, and reversibility.
6. Invite missing evidence, affected perspectives, and dissent without relitigating excluded scope.
7. Apply the stated decision method and ask the owner to make the outcome explicit.
8. Record selection, rationale, rejected options, risk acceptance, actions, communication, and revisit trigger.
9. Confirm understanding and publish the decision record.

## Failure plan

- Do not manufacture consensus or let seniority silently replace the stated authority.
- Never ask affected participants to endorse a decision already made without disclosing that boundary.
- If evidence or authority is insufficient, choose a bounded evidence action rather than a pretend decision.
- Route confidential personnel, legal, security, and commercial details appropriately.

## Done

- The decision, authority, criteria, options, evidence, and method are confirmed
- Outcome, rationale, dissent, actions, and revisit conditions are recorded
- Affected owners acknowledge the published decision

# write-an-academic-cv

Use the institution's criteria and distinguish status, role, and contribution precisely.

## When to use

- Use for comprehensive academic evaluation rather than a short general resume.
- Do not hide corrections, duplicate outputs, mislabel teaching responsibility, or claim institutional service without evidence.

## Procedure

1. Obtain the current official criteria, required sections, date range, citation format, and review audience.
2. Build a dated master inventory of appointments, education, research, teaching, mentoring, funding, service, awards, and impact.
3. Verify publication, preprint, acceptance, revision, submission, correction, and retraction status.
4. Record teaching role, course, enrollment, level, dates, responsibility, and evidence without exposing student data.
5. Distinguish institutional, disciplinary, public, clinical, and community service plus the candidate's role.
6. State grant position, award status, amount basis, period, and collaborators accurately.
7. Organize within the institution's categories and explain nonstandard outputs or career interruptions proportionately.
8. Reconcile totals and dates with institutional systems, ORCID, repositories, and supporting statements.
9. Produce a clean CV document and a private evidence index for review.

## Done

- An academic CV aligns verified research, teaching, mentoring, funding, service, and recognition with current criteria
- Output status, roles, dates, totals, identifiers, student privacy, and institutional records are reconciled

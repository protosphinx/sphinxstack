# tailor-a-resume-for-a-leadership-role

Show leadership through accountable decisions and outcomes, not title inflation.

## When to use

- Use with a real role description and a verified base resume.
- Do not invent headcount, budget, reporting lines, strategy ownership, board exposure, or metrics.

## Procedure

1. Extract target mandate, level, scope, leadership behaviors, domain, stakeholders, outcomes, and required qualifications.
2. Verify candidate titles, reporting lines, team scope, budget, geography, decisions, programs, and results.
3. Select evidence for direction, prioritization, hiring, coaching, delivery, change, governance, crisis, influence, and learning.
4. Rewrite summary and bullets around context, leadership action, scale, outcome, and evidence.
5. Distinguish direct authority, matrix influence, contribution, sponsorship, and team outcome precisely.
6. Prioritize the most relevant recent work and remove unsupported or junior task detail.
7. Check chronology, claims, confidentiality, keywords, readability, accessibility, and role fit.
8. Obtain candidate approval and preserve a verified master.

## Done

- A tailored leadership resume document records target requirements, verified scope, decisions, team context, outcomes, governance, and source evidence
- Title, headcount, budget, authority, influence, metric, chronology, keyword, confidentiality, and candidate-approval checks verify truth

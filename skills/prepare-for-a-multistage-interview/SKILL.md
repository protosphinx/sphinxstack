# prepare-for-a-multistage-interview
## When to use
- Use for loops, panels, cases, presentations, technical rounds, or executive stages.
- Do not memorize invented stories or infer protected personal details about interviewers.
## Procedure
1. Verify stages, criteria, participants, format, timing, access, and allowed tools.
2. Map requirements to varied verified examples.
3. Prepare concise stories with context, action, outcome, evidence, and limits.
4. Rehearse technical, behavioral, case, presentation, and question transitions.
5. Prepare stage-specific questions without repeating public facts.
6. Test logistics and update notes after each stage without exposing confidential content.
## Failure plan
- Ask for clarification or accommodation when a stage is inaccessible or ambiguous.
## Worked example
A candidate uses different evidence for architecture, collaboration, and executive discussions rather than repeating one story.
## Done
- An interview plan records stages, criteria, evidence, questions, logistics, access, rehearsal, and learning
- Requirement, source, variety, timing, format, confidentiality, accessibility, and no-invention checks verify readiness

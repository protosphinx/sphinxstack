# write-a-government-resume

Follow the specific agency's current instructions rather than generic resume conventions.

## When to use

- Use when an application requires detailed duties, hours, grades, classifications, or criterion statements.
- Do not infer eligibility, clearance, veterans preference, citizenship status, or classification equivalence.

## Procedure

1. Retrieve the current official vacancy, applicant guide, eligibility rules, assessment method, and deadline.
2. Record every required field, document, declaration, format, page rule, and submission step.
3. Map specialized experience and competencies to verified examples at the required duration and level.
4. Include employer, title, dates, hours, salary or grade, supervisor contact preference, and duties only as required.
5. Translate private-sector terminology without changing scope, authority, or outcomes.
6. Address each criterion with situation, action, result, and fair individual attribution.
7. Verify education, licenses, preference, service, clearance, and eligibility documents through authorized evidence.
8. Review privacy and redact only where official rules permit.
9. Preview the portal, save submission proof, and retain the exact package.

## Done

- A government resume and application matrix answer every official experience, competency, eligibility, and file requirement
- Dates, hours, grades, credentials, preference claims, attachments, and portal submission are checked against current official evidence

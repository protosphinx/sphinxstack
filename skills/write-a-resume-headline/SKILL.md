# write-a-resume-headline

Use evidence and recognizable role language rather than a slogan.

## When to use

- Use beneath a candidate name or at the start of a profile.
- Do not claim a title, credential, clearance, or level the candidate does not hold.

## Procedure

1. Identify the target role, seniority, domain, audience, and wording used in the job description.
2. Inventory verified experience, specialty, industry context, credentials, and strongest relevant outcome.
3. Choose one role label the candidate can defend.
4. Add one or two differentiators that the experience section proves.
5. Remove first-person language, empty adjectives, keyword strings, objectives, and private details.
6. Keep the line scannable and avoid repeating the contact block or next sentence.
7. Compare it with the target role and test whether every phrase is supported elsewhere.

## Worked example

Instead of “Visionary results-driven ninja,” a candidate with verified platform and reliability work uses “Platform engineer | Kubernetes reliability and developer tooling.” Both specialties are demonstrated in later bullets.

## Done

- A headline draft names the target role and evidence-backed differentiators in one scannable line
- Every title, credential, specialty, and level is checked against the resume and source record

# thank-an-interviewer

Reference the conversation instead of sending a generic performance.

## When to use

- Use within the expected local business rhythm after an interview.
- Send separate notes only when each recipient provided distinct context and direct contact is appropriate.

## Procedure

1. Verify each email recipient's name, role, address, and preferred communication path.
2. Review notes and choose one specific role, team, problem, or success detail actually discussed.
3. Thank the interviewer for their time and connect the detail to truthful continued interest.
4. Clarify one answer or attach one promised item only if it adds material value.
5. Keep the note short and do not repeat the resume, flatter excessively, or pressure for a decision.
6. Check names, company, role, attachments, permissions, and links.
7. Send once, then track the stated next-step date rather than immediately following again.

## Done

- A sent note references a genuine interview detail, confirms the candidate's current interest, and fulfills any promised follow-up
- Recipient, role, names, links, attachments, and timing were verified before delivery

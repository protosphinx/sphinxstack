# create-an-editorial-calendar

Plan what can be published well with the available evidence and capacity. Give every item a purpose,
owner, source, production path, review gate, and reuse plan.

## Inputs

- Name the audience, publication goals, channels, cadence, owners, and planning horizon.
- Gather existing content, search and support questions, campaign dates, source expertise, and performance evidence.
- Record approval, legal, accessibility, brand, and privacy requirements.

## Procedure

1. Translate goals into audience questions and observable outcomes such as qualified visits, replies, use, or retention.
2. Audit existing content for accuracy, performance, gaps, duplication, and material worth updating.
3. Create content pillars broad enough to sustain several useful pieces and narrow enough to guide selection.
4. Build a backlog with working title, audience question, format, evidence, owner, effort, channel, and intended next action.
5. Rank items by audience value, evidence readiness, timing, reuse, and production cost.
6. Estimate real capacity across research, drafting, design, review, accessibility, publishing, and distribution.
7. Schedule a mix of durable, timely, update, and experimental work without exceeding capacity.
8. Assign brief, draft, review, final, publish, distribute, and update dates plus one accountable owner.
9. Define review gates for factual, legal, brand, accessibility, and channel-specific requirements.
10. Plan contextual links, derivative formats, and distribution while preserving the original meaning.
11. Review actual effort and outcomes each cycle, then move, cut, or update the calendar.

## Boundaries

Do not invent expertise, evidence, publication certainty, or search volume. Never schedule use of private
stories, customer material, or personal data without permission. Leave capacity for corrections.

## Done

- A calendar connects each scheduled item to an audience question, evidence source, owner, channel, and outcome
- Workload includes research, production, review, accessibility, publishing, distribution, and updates
- Review gates and permissions are assigned before deadlines
- A completed cycle records actual effort, publication state, outcome evidence, and calendar changes

Then use blog-post, newsletter-issue, social-post, or video-script for individual pieces.

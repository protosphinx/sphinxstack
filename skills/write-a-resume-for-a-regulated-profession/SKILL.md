# write-a-resume-for-a-regulated-profession

State credentials and authorized scope exactly.

## When to use

- Use with current official role and credential requirements plus verified candidate records.
- Do not imply an active license, specialty, jurisdiction, clearance, or independent scope that is absent, expired, restricted, or supervised.

## Procedure

1. Confirm target role, jurisdiction, regulator, current official requirements, employer needs, and candidate authorization.
2. Verify license name, number handling, issuer, jurisdiction, status, dates, restrictions, and source.
3. Verify education, accredited training, supervised hours, registration, certification, continuing education, and insurance where relevant.
4. Map work history to permitted scope, populations, settings, procedures, standards, quality, safety, and outcomes.
5. Distinguish independent, supervised, provisional, trainee, delegated, and team-based work.
6. Handle investigations, gaps, restrictions, sanctions, confidential cases, and expired credentials truthfully with qualified advice where needed.
7. Format for role, country, ATS, accessibility, privacy, and document requirements.
8. Recheck current credential status and obtain candidate approval before submission.

## Done

- A regulated-profession resume records target role, verified credentials, jurisdiction, scope, supervision, experience, compliance, and evidence
- Official-requirement, license-status, date, scope, supervision, training, confidentiality, format, and candidate-approval checks verify accuracy

# follow-up-after-applying

Help someone follow up on applications without becoming
a pest. One well-timed, well-written nudge signals exactly the
reliability entry-level employers want, and a second message rarely
helps; the skill is knowing which situation calls for which. The
bar: every due follow-up handled today — sent, scheduled, or
deliberately closed — with the tracker updated to match.

## Ground rules

- One follow-up per application, two only when something concrete
  changed (an interview happened, they were told to check back on a
  date). Silence after that means no; say so plainly and point
  their energy at the next application.
- Their voice, their send. You draft, they edit until it sounds
  like them, and they send from their own account. You never
  contact an employer as them, by any channel.
- Never manufacture a pretext. No fake "just confirming you got my
  materials", no invented deadlines from other offers. The honest
  version — still interested, checking on timing — is fine.

## Triage

Open their tracker (job-application-tracker builds one if none
exists) and sort what is due. The timing rules: online application,
nudge at 5–7 business days; walk-in where a manager took their
resume, 3–5 days, by the channel the manager indicated; after an
interview, thank-you same evening, then a status check only after
the timeline they were given expires; posting says "no calls" —
respect it, no follow-up at all. Anything already nudged once with
silence for two weeks gets marked closed, out loud, so they hear
that closing is normal and not failure.

## Draft and send

For each due item, pick the channel the thread is already on and
draft three or four sentences: name and the role applied for, date
applied, one line of continued interest with a true specific
("I'm still very interested — especially the weekend shifts"), a
soft close ("I understand you're busy — thanks for your time").
No guilt, no essay, no demands for a decision. Phone-call cases —
some managers say "call me" — get a fifteen-second spoken version
rehearsed out loud instead. They send each message while you are
together, and each send goes in the tracker with the date.

## Update the board

After the pass: statuses current, closed items marked closed, any
"check back next month" promises given real calendar dates. End by
counting active applications — if the number is thin, the next move
is more applications (find-openings, apply-online), because
follow-ups tend pipeline but only new applications fill it.

## Done

- Every due follow-up sent, scheduled, or closed on purpose
- All messages in their voice, sent by them, logged
- Tracker current, and the next move named if the pipeline is thin

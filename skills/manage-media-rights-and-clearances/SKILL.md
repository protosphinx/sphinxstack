# manage-media-rights-and-clearances

Make every published element traceable to authority for the actual territory, channel, duration, and edit.

## Procedure

1. Inventory footage, images, music, recordings, text, brands, locations, performances, and likenesses.
2. Record source, creator, claimant, file identifier, first use, and production owner.
3. Determine copyright, license, release, contract, public-domain, or exception basis with qualified review.
4. Specify channels, territories, duration, versions, edits, promotion, archive, and sublicensing.
5. Track fees, credits, notices, cue sheets, restrictions, expiry, and renewal.
6. Link executed evidence to the exact asset and final use.
7. Flag unresolved, disputed, restricted, generative, or replaceable material.
8. Review the locked master against the register before delivery.
9. Archive evidence and schedule expiry or takedown actions.

## Guardrails

- This workflow does not substitute for qualified legal advice.
- Possession, online availability, or attribution alone does not prove permission.
- Never broaden a license beyond its written scope.

## Done

- A rights and clearance register lists every relevant asset and use
- Executed evidence, credits, restrictions, territory, channel, and expiry are verified
- The locked master reconciles with cleared assets and unresolved items

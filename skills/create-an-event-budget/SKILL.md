# create-an-event-budget

## Procedure

1. Confirm scope, capacity, currency, tax basis, funding source, authority, and reporting period.
2. Estimate venue, production, access, speakers, travel, staffing, catering, security, insurance, marketing, registration, and closeout cost.
3. Separate fixed, variable, committed, quoted, estimated, donated, and contingent amounts.
4. Model attendance, ticket, sponsorship, grant, and other income with explicit assumptions.
5. Include deposits, payment dates, cancellation exposure, cash timing, and a risk-based contingency.
6. Build base, lower-demand, delay, cancellation, and high-cost scenarios.
7. Assign budget owners, approval thresholds, purchase controls, and reconciliation cadence.
8. Record changes and update forecast against actuals and commitments.

## Guardrails

- Do not count uncontracted sponsorship or optimistic ticket sales as secured income.
- Never omit accessibility, safety, cancellation, tax, or payment-processing cost.
- Route tax, accounting, insurance, contract, and employment questions to qualified owners.

## Done

- A budget model reconciles planned cost, secured income, commitments, cash timing, and contingency
- Assumptions, scenarios, owners, approvals, and material uncertainty are recorded
- Forecast and change checks are scheduled

# prepare-for-a-behavioral-interview

## Procedure

1. Map likely competencies and outcomes from the role, process, and available company evidence.
2. Select varied examples from the achievement record with different contexts, strengths, and stakes.
3. For each story, outline situation, responsibility, actions, reasoning, collaboration, result, and learning.
4. Make individual contribution clear without erasing the team.
5. Preserve uncertainty, setbacks, changed decisions, and unresolved outcomes honestly.
6. Redact confidential details while retaining enough specificity to be credible.
7. Practice answering different questions from the same evidence rather than memorizing scripts.
8. Record gaps and prepare concise clarifying questions when a prompt is ambiguous.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent stories, metrics, conflict, customers, responsibility, failure, or resolution.
- Never disclose protected employer, customer, security, health, or employee information.
- Do not frame ordinary collaboration as solo heroism.

## Done

- A story-bank document maps target competencies to sourced examples and individual contribution
- Each story includes actions, reasoning, outcome, boundaries, and learning
- Timed practice is recorded and feedback checks confirm accuracy and adaptability

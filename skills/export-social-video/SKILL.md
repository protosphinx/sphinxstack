# export-social-video

Create intentional variants from an approved master instead of blindly cropping one file.

## Procedure

1. Confirm final edit, rights, captions, graphics, platform, placement, and delivery deadline.
2. Define aspect ratio, frame size, frame rate, duration, codec, bitrate, and file limit per variant.
3. Reframe shots and graphics while preserving faces, actions, text, and safe areas.
4. Check opening comprehension, pacing, loop, call to action, and muted viewing.
5. Add burned or sidecar captions according to platform capability.
6. Mix and encode to the required loudness and channel layout.
7. Export from the approved timeline with stable names and version metadata.
8. Watch every encoded file beginning to end on representative devices.
9. Save a delivery report with checksums and upload status.

## Guardrails

- Do not crop context or captions in a way that changes meaning.
- Never assume the platform preview matches the uploaded encode.
- Keep masters separate from compressed delivery files.

## Done

- A delivery package contains approved platform variants and captions
- Framing, text, loudness, encoding, and full playback are verified
- Checksums, version, and upload status are recorded

# manage-an-editorial-corrections-process

Make correction proportional, visible, and traceable without repeating harm.

## When to use

- Use for articles, reports, newsletters, documentation, audio, video, social posts, and syndicated content.
- Do not quietly overwrite a material error or expose a complainant or source unnecessarily.

## Procedure

1. Provide a safe intake route and record claim, content version, location, evidence, affected people, contact, urgency, and conflict.
2. Preserve published and source versions, editorial history, distribution, and access.
3. Assign independent fact, identity, data, translation, rights, legal, safety, and editorial review as needed.
4. Classify typo, clarification, update, correction, editor's note, retraction, recall, and urgent harm.
5. Decide wording, prominence, timestamp, source links, conclusion impact, and affected-person contact.
6. Propagate to pages, feeds, newsletters, translations, captions, transcripts, syndication, social, search, archives, and partners.
7. Verify every destination and record unreachable or cached copies.
8. Monitor harm, appeals, repeated errors, timeliness, and control remediation.

## Done

- A corrections register records intake, evidence, versions, classification, decision, wording, distribution, verification, appeals, and prevention
- Source, identity, translation, derivative, timestamp, prominence, destination, affected-person, and correction-visibility checks verify completion

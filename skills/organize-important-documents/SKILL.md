# organize-important-documents

Organize for retrieval under stress. An index can reveal where a record lives without placing every sensitive document in one vulnerable folder.

## When to use

- Use for identity, household, financial, insurance, property, education, employment, health, legal, tax, and emergency records.
- Follow applicable retention and professional guidance for regulated, legal, medical, or tax documents.

## Procedure

1. Define who needs access, common retrieval scenarios, privacy risks, and the systems already in use.
2. Inventory records by category, owner, issuer, date, status, physical location, digital location, and retention rule.
3. Create a simple naming convention using document type, relevant date, person or asset, and version.
4. Separate active, archival, expired, pending, and safe-to-destroy records.
5. Keep an index with locations and review dates; avoid copying full identifiers into the index.
6. Scan only useful records at legible quality and verify completeness before relying on the copy.
7. Encrypt sensitive digital storage, use strong access control, and protect physical originals.
8. Create an authorized emergency-access method and an offsite backup appropriate to the threat model.
9. Test retrieval, restore, and handoff; schedule regular inbox processing and retention review.
10. Destroy approved records with a method appropriate to their sensitivity and log material disposal.

## Failure plan

- Do not discard originals merely because a scan exists without checking whether the original is required.
- Do not centralize passwords, full account numbers, and identity documents in an unprotected index.
- If ownership or retention is uncertain, quarantine the record for review.
- Avoid elaborate taxonomies that the household will not maintain.

## Done

- Important records are indexed, findable, access-controlled, and backed up
- Originals, copies, versions, retention, and disposal status are distinguishable
- Retrieval and emergency access have been tested without exposing unnecessary data
